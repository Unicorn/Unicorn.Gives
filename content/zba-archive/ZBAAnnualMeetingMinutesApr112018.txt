                     Lincoln Township Zoning Board of Appeals
                                 Annual Meeting
                             Wednesday, April 11, 2018

The meeting was called to order by Vice-Chair Swanson at 7:00 pm.

Roll Call:
Present – Board Members: Bridges, Engelhardt, McCaslin, Swanson
Staff: Zoning Administrator Abbott, Recording Secretary Sherrod
Absent – Alt. Kim Smith, Building Inspector Mantei, excused
Public - 2

Approval of Minutes:
Regular Meeting minutes of March 14, 2018 – It was moved by Engelhardt and
seconded by McCaslin to accept the minutes as written. Motion carried with no
objections.

Election of Officers: Prior to the action of election of officers, Engelhardt voiced
a concern that the alternate is not present, leaving only 4 members present. She
felt that no action should be taken without contacting the Board of Commissioners
to have them appoint a new member. She also raised the concern that the two
members who are designated as Liaison are not allowed to vote according to the
ZBA Bylaws. Clerk Majewski explained that the term Liaison as referenced in the
ZBA Bylaws does not refer to the type of liaison members on the ZBA as they are
Zoning Board of Appeals Minutes           2                                 4/11/2018


the ex officio members with full rights of voting and serve as representatives of the
Township Board of Trustees and the Township Planning Commission.

Engelhardt then made a motion to contact the township board to request that they
appoint members in order to bring the ZBA to full membership. McCaslin
seconded the motion. Motion passed with all in favor.

New Business:
1. Discussion of 2017 Ordinance: several comments were made.
2. Review of Open Meeting Conduct: Engelhardt read from an article about public
meeting conduct. See Attachment #1.

Old Business:
1. Response from Township Lawyer re: March 14, 2018, Drew Hearing
      Engelhardt made a motion that due to the Drew hearing not being handled
pursuant to statute the ZBA rehear the variance request without further cost to the
Drews if the Drews so desire. Swanson seconded. Swanson: Yes; Engelhardt:
Yes; Bridges: Yes; McCaslin: Yes. Motion passed 4 to 0.

2. Update of Finding of Fact Worksheet
       Engelhardt suggested some new wording to make the worksheet more usable
for the ZBA. She asked them to look it over and give input. The decision was to
make sure that the language is consistent across all the forms including the one
used by the applicant, the one used by the ZBA, and the language in the ordinance.
Engelhardt volunteered to work on this and refer it back to the full ZBA for
approval. Clerk Majewski will make sure that it meets the approval of the
township attorney. After full approval and adoption, the forms will be updated on
the webpage.

Public Comment: None

Questions & Comments by ZBA Members: None

Adjournment: Swanson moved to adjourn; Engelhardt seconded. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:54 pm.

Respectfully Submitted,

Cindy Engelhardt, Secretary
