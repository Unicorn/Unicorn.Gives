Purpose: Chair Godau read the following information: This is a variance request
by Allstate Sign Co., Inc. pertaining to the future Dollar General Store located at
the SW corner of Arthur Rd. and Lake George Ave., Lake George, MI 48633,
where as a Variance is requested to exceed dimensional and height limits for signs
as permitted in Section 1517 of the Lincoln Township Zoning Ordinance. The
notice of this variance was published in the Clare County Cleaver on April 30,
2015, in accordance with the Michigan Zoning Enabling Act 2006. Notifications
were sent to all property owners within 300 feet of the property for the purpose of
public comment being made to the ZBA. The public hearing information was also
posted at the township hall and on the township’s website in the calendar of events.

Brief Explanation of the Rules: Chair Godau explained that variance requests are
submitted by the persons making the request. The Lincoln Township Board of
Appeals must follow the variance standards created by Michigan Statute. These
provide that the ZBA must determine that a Practical Difficulty exists in order to
grant a variance. These statutes ARE LAW and provide that: 1. There is a
Practical Difficulty; 2. The circumstances are Unique to the Property; 3. There is
Substantial Justice; 4. The issue was Not Self-Created; 5. Minimum Variance
Necessary

Attachment #1


                             (Zoning Administrator's Report)
Our Zoning requires one wall sign per street frontage is allowed for
establishments and the wall signs shall not exceed an area of forty (40) square
feet. It also requires one freestanding per street frontage is permitted and the
freestanding signs shall not exceed a height of sixteen (16) feet and an area of
one-hundred (100) square feet.
The ZBA should grant a variance from the township's zoning laws only after the
property in question meets stringent tests described in the zoning ordinance. Only
the rarest situations should qualify for relief from the zoning ordinance's
requirements because the zoning regulations are ultimately intended to protect the
general health, safety and welfare of the township.
Practical Difficulty: Lincoln Township has a set of criteria an applicant must meet to
be allowed a Variance. An applicant must meet each of the 5 criteria's for a variance,
not 4 or 3, all 5 practical difficulties. Michigan appellate court has held that the
Zoning Board of Appeals cannot grant a Variance without substantial evidence of all
5 practical difficulties. The Michigan Court of Appeals has also adopted the
following factor, whether compliance with the strict letter of the restrictions
governing area, setbacks, frontage, height, bulk or density would unreasonably
prevent the owner from using the property for a permitted purpose.


For number one they are asking for nothing more than their typical signage. It
might be typical signage but it is in violation of our Zoning Ordinance and offers
no practical difficulty. They talk about visibility, we are a small town, and they
are the only big store in town.
Number 2, 3, 4, and 5 all talk about height, visibility and the intersection. As the
Dollar General is the only store of this type in the area none of these offer a
Practical Difficulty.
