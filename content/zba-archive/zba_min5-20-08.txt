                    Administrator Lambert
           Public Attendees: 0

Purpose: To elect officers and set an agenda for the June 2, 2008 meeting.

New Business:

   (Y) 3 (N) 0 M otion Passed.

An explanation was presented for how the list for mailing was derived for the upcoming
variance hearing. Lambert stated that any mail received is read even if the person does
not own property within the 300 ft. The most important issue is the ordinance in
question. The group will go separately to the residence to see the property rather than
together. Everyone is required to go. The ordinances are in the process of being revised
to indicate the new number of ZBA board members. Reinke asked if there any alternates
for the ZBA? Chapman explained that any alternate for the planning commission seat is
supplied by the Planning Commission. Chair Smith will investigate alternates for the
other two seats on the ZBA.
