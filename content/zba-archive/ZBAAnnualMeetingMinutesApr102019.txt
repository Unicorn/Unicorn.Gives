                     Lincoln Township Zoning Board of Appeals
                                 Annual Meeting
                             Wednesday, April 10, 2019

The meeting was called to order by Chair Carey at 7:00 pm.

Roll Call:
Present – Board Members: Carey, Engelhardt, McCaslin, Beougher
Staff: Recording Secretary Sherrod
Absent – Bridges (excused), Swanson
Public - None

Approval of Agenda: McCaslin moved to accept the agenda as written;
Engelhardt seconded. Motion carried.

Approval of Minutes:
Regular Meeting minutes of March 13, 2019 –Carey moved to accept the minutes
as written; Engelhardt seconded. Motion carried.

Public Comment: None

Election of Officers:
Zoning Board of Appeals Minutes         2                                4/10/2019


Engelhardt moved to accept the current officers: Chair Roger Carey, Vice-Chair
Marshall Swanson, and Secretary Cynthia Engelhardt; McCaslin seconded.
Motion carried with all in favor. Slate of officers remains the same.


Questions & Comments by ZBA Members: None

Bylaw Changes:
Chair Carey presented the new bylaws which contain five changes that he is
proposing. Engelhardt made a motion to replace the April 13, 2016, bylaws with
the new set of bylaws; McCaslin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor by
voice vote.

Public Comment: None

Adjournment: Carey moved to adjourn; Engelhardt seconded. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:20 pm.

Respectfully Submitted,

Cindy Engelhardt, Secretary
