Resident filed for a zoning permit on date January 20, 2019, by Jeffery and Sandra

The following is Jeffery and Sandra Drew’s reason for Appeal:
Regarding: Appeal of Decision of the Zoning Administrator

We wish to appeal the decision of the Lincoln Township Zoning Administrator,
dated December 6, 2018, denying our request for a zoning permit to construct a
deck on our property at Lot 1, Block 1, of Oakdale Resort. The application for a
zoning permit dated November 9, 2018, included all necessary supporting
documentation for an accessory structure on our property referenced above in
accordance with Section 20.9 Accessory Structures and Buildings. The proposed
accessory structure meets all the requirements of this section.

There are so many conflicting sections in the Lincoln Township Zoning Ordinance,
some of which Ms Abbott referenced in her letter of denial regarding the
placement of decks and other structures, that judging from all the other accessory
structures along Lake George, Section 20.9 is the proper way to address the
permitting of our deck. We are certain that the requirements in Section 20.9 do
pertain in our situation as a water front lot and supersede other sections of the
zoning ordinance referenced by Ms Abbott. Our logic for this is laid out in our
application letter of 9 November 2018, attached.

We refer to the Lincoln Township attorney's letter of February 2018 regarding the
merging of our lots that was agreed to by Lincoln Township when the Lot 1, Block
1 Oakdale Resort lots were created. The township has treated the lots as a "total
parcel" since that time.

For two years we tried to meet requirements and the spirit of the Lincoln Township
Zoning Ordinance. We believe that construction of this modest 120 square foot
Zoning Board of Appeals Minutes           6                                 3/13/2019


deck will not in any way diminish the health safety, or welfare of the township, nor
will it negatively affect the township's natural resources. As all the other decks,
docks, and other accessory structures along Lake George, it will contribute to and
enhance the aesthetic and economic values of the community.

We are prepared to rebut each of the reasons for denial by Ms Abbott at the Zoning
Board of Appeals hearing if this is necessary.

Jeffery and Sandra Drew

walls, or any other supports, which is used for housing, sheltering, storing, or
enclosing persons, animals, or personal property, or carrying on business or other
activities. This definition includes but is not limited to dwellings, garages, and
greenhouses, and anything that exceeds sixty (60) sq. ft. in floor area or eight (8)

attachment to something having permanent location on the ground.

That said, in your case you could have an accessory building 10 feet from the

As for your deck application, the definition of deck is: Deck: An exterior floor
system or platform, either attached or unattached to a building, and may be
supported by posts, piers or other independent supports, the surface elevation of
which at any point is higher than the surrounding ground elevation.
Structure: Anything constructed or erected, the use of which requires permanent
location on the ground or attachment to something having such location on the
ground.
