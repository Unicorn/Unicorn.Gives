Conflict of Interest Statement (Declare and Cite): No conflict of interest was

General Comments by Chair: Chair Godau explained the structure and functions
of the township government. He went on to explain what a variance involves and
how the zoning administrator deals with the steps of zoning permits, building
permits, and then the process in which a permit is denied. He also explained the
Zoning Board of Appeals Minutes          2                                   5/10/17


roles of the zoning administrator and the building inspector. The decision of the
zoning board of appeals shall be final.

A party aggrieved by the decision may appeal to the circuit court of Clare County
within whichever of the following deadlines comes first:
   a. Thirty days after the zoning board of appeals issues its decision in writing
      signed by the chairperson, if there is a chairperson, or signed by the
      members of the zoning board of appeals, if there is no chairperson.
   b. Twenty-one days after the zoning board of appeals approves the minutes of
      its decision.

Purpose: Chair Godau read the following information: This is a variance request
from Robert and Susan Guzowski regarding their property at 2568 Cedar Rd.,
Lincoln Township, Harrison, MI, 48625. The variance request was submitted on
March 22, 2017.

They are seeking approval to build a 32X48X14 Pole Barn at a setback less than
the required 90 feet from the road-right-of-way and larger in square footage than
the current Township Zoning allows in an R-R zoning district.

Also they would exceed the maximum allowed accessory buildings (not to exceed
3) where the combined square footage of all accessory buildings shall not exceed
twenty-four (2,400) square feet.

Public notice: Notice of this Public Hearing was posted in the Clare County
Cleaver (Legal Section) on April 20, 2017. Notifications were sent to all property
owners within 300 feet of the property for the purpose of public comment being
made to the ZBA. The public hearing information was also posted at the township
hall and on the township’s website in the calendar of events.

Brief Explanation of the Rules: Chair Godau explained that variance requests are
submitted in person by the persons making the request. The Lincoln Township
Board of Appeals must follow the variance standards created by Michigan Statute.
These provide that the ZBA must determine that a Practical Difficulty exists in
order to grant a variance. These statutes ARE LAW and provide that: 1. There is a
Practical Difficulty; 2. The circumstances are Unique to the Property; 3. There is
Substantial Justice; 4. The issue was Not Self-Created; 5. Minimum Variance
Necessary

ZBA Members Comments & Questions: Tami McCaslin presented information
concerning the new Zoning Ordinances. She stated that the Planning Commission
does have concerns about 20 acre and larger parcels in the R-R District having
more leeway in the size and number of their accessory buildings. She read the
proposed changes made by Mark Eidelson, LandPlan Consultant. She explained
that the Planning Commission has sent their opinion on these back to the Township
Board.

She wanted to bring this to the attention of the ZBA because if this ordinance was
already in place, the Guzowskis would not need to be here tonight. She also stated
that she would not tear down the little cabin for a million dollars if it were on her
property.

Engelhardt read a statement from the state which says in some cases of variance
requests the ZBA just needs to use their common sense and good judgment to
resolve an issue fairly.

Bridges stated as a member of the Township Board that he doesn’t know when the
new ordinance will be approved. He is bothered that this concerns an R-R district
of 30 acres and that one more building won’t hurt the property. If it had been
zoned as agricultural, there would be no need for permits. He stated that the ZBA
can’t decide on historical or esthetic value. He agrees with McCaslin’s statement.
He also would not remove the historical log home on the property. He feels that
they have a situation that does not apply to other R-R lots.

McCaslin thanked the Guzowskis for their patience and their welcome to their
property as well as their input at these meetings.

   Robert & Sue Guzowski May 10, 2017 Zoning Variance hearing on
                        Accessory Buildings
Practical Difficulty

        I can meet 90 foot setback from road right of way for building and withdraw the setback
        request. The strict compliance of ordinance 1502 subsection 4 is unnecessarily
        burdensome for us. The burden being that to fall in compliance with this ordinance two
        existing buildings would need to be destroyed, and denies land owner a permitted right.

    1. Unique Situation
       This property is zoned R/R and is a 20 acre parcel, we also own the 10 acres to the west
       and the property lines are contiguous. The unique situation posed to us is the two
       buildings are of small square footage and date back to 1906, and one of the buildings a
       log cabin has family value, Sues Grandfather Homesteaded this property and resided in
       the log cabin. The historic value to the Township is displayed in a painting on the very
       walls we are holding this meeting. It has always given us great pride that we have been
       able to preserve this structure. This condition is unique to our property and not shared
       by any of our immediate surrounding property.

    2. Substantial Justice
       Granting of this variance for an additional accessory building and square footage would
       not cause any detrimental value or public safety concerns for adjoining properties. I
       would argue that it would increase the public safety and value of property by allowing
       property owner to keep all his belongings inside a building, and not contributing to any
       blight on the property, nor cause future land owners any detriment.

    3. Practical Difficulty
       We did not create this practical difficulty. The two structures in question have been on
       this property before zoning ordinance was passed.

    4. Minimum Variance Necessary

       The minimum square footage allowed for accessory buildings in Article XV, Section 1502
       is restrictive to all land owners zoned R/R who have a minimum of 10 acres. The

       protecting the surrounding property from any blight conditions.
