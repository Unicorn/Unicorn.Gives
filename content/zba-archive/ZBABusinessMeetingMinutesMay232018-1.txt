                                Business Meeting

Pledge Of Allegiance: Swanson led the pledge.

Call To Order: Swanson called the meeting to order at 1900.

Roll Call:
Present – Board Members: Engelhardt, McCaslin, Swanson, Carey
Staff: Zoning Administrator Abbott
Absent – Alt. Kim Smith, Township Board Rep. Jerry Bridges

Approval of Minutes:
Annual Meeting minutes of April 11, 2018 – It was moved by McCaslin and
seconded by Engelhardt to accept the minutes as written. Motion carried with no
objections.

Announcement Of New Board Appointee: Engelhardt read the announcement of
Roger Carey as the new ZBA appointee as presented at the May 14 Township
Meeting.
Zoning Board of Appeals Minutes          2                                 5/23/2018


Election of Officers: McCaslin nominated Roger Carey as Chairperson for the
ZBA. Motion seconded by Engelhardt. Motion passed with no objection.
Carry moved the positions of Vice Chair and Secretary remain the same provided
Swanson and Engelhardt are willing to continue in their present positions. Motion
seconded by McCaslin. Motion passed with no objection.

Review Of Bylaws: Section 5 of the Bylaws was reviewed referencing Article 8
Section 8.2 of the Lincoln Township Zoning Ordinance concluding that: The ZBA
is to have five voting members appointed by the Township Board: Three persons
from the general population, one Planning Commission member and one Township
Board member.

Responsibilities of elected officers were reviewed.

Preparing Variance Information: After review of Article 8 of the Lincoln
Township Zoning Ordinance and discussion it was concluded: the ZBA Chairman
will coordinate with the Zoning Administrator to prepare variance packets for the
ZBA.

Review Of Fact Finding Sheet: A Fact Finding document, which had been
reviewed by the Township lawyer, was presented to the group by Engelhardt. It
reflected the seven indicators for a variance request found in the Zoning Ordinance
and the form used by variance applicants. Per the suggestion of the lawyer it did
not include a yes or no compliance notation, only a comments area.

Carey presented information from Brad Neumann, Government and Public Policy
Educator, MSU stating… (The ZBA) must find all practical difficulties yes to deny
the variance. With this in mind Carey would like a yes or no to be on the work
sheet as well comments.

McCaslin emphasized the need to keep the fact finding sheet compliant with the
Ordinance and the applicants form. She stated it may be difficult to have the
ZBA’s fact finding sheet questions stated as yes or no. Carey stated it would be
preferable to include yes or no on the sheet in the event of a court case.

It was concluded Engelhardt will electronically send the form as presented at the
meeting to Carey for him to carry forward with the issue.

Public Comment: None

Comments/Questions By ZBA Members: There was lengthy discussion
regarding how to review a variance request stressing looking at all potential areas
within the Ordinance. Engelhardt read the announcement made at the May 14
Township Meeting regarding the ZBA March 14, 2018, Variance Request.

McCaslin reminded those present there is a Planning/Zoning training session
Thursday, August 16. If interested, we are to contact Carol Majewski.

Adjournment: Swanson moved to adjourn; Engelhardt seconded. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 2030.
