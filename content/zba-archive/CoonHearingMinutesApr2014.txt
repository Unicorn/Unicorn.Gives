Purpose: To consider the application of Ken and Mike Coon for a Request for
Variance for property #010-324-008-00 and 010-324-009-00 located at 1434 Lake
Drive, Lake George, Michigan. Property owners request approval to construct a
new residence which would violate the current rear and front set-backs and the
overall lot dimension. The notice of this variance was published in the Clare
County Cleaver in the week of March 27, 2014, in accordance with the township
zoning ordinances.

Chair Swanson explained the rules and procedures of the ZBA.

Correspondence: All members of the Board received communications in support
of the Coon family and all read those statements.

Petitioner’s Statement: Mike Coon, representing himself and his family, stated
that they desire to build a cottage on Lake George which will continue a family
tradition. They have 6 lots on Lake Drive in the family. They want to join the two
properties that are on the lake and then build a cottage which will span the two lots.
This will give them a continuous lake frontage. The existing structure is 45 feet
from the water’s edge. They are asking for the same variance for the new cottage
and also to be able to put a 12 foot deck on the lakeside. Also they want to have
another 7 foot variance on the rear, as well as another variance which will allow an
additional 1% square footage.

Past variances granted in the same neighborhood have created a basis for these
requests. They are asking for the same consideration given to their neighbors in
the past.

His brother stated as well that this will also center the structure in the two pieces of
property, creating a less crowded, more aesthetic effect in that area.

Township Officials’ Statements:
Zoning Administrator Carey stated that the reason for his ruling in denial of the
initial request was for violations of setback and violations of lot dimension.

Public Comment: Engelhardt stated that the mistakes may have been made in the
past and the present board is trying to avoid more of those mistakes in the spirit of
preserving the lake and its surroundings.

Mike Coon’s mother, Sally, stated that she feels that her family has gone all out to
make sure that their property looks nice and will be well cared for. They have the
neighbors’ support that none of the variances asked for will be objected to.

Engelhardt stated that the board did receive supportive statements from the
neighbors.

Mike Coon said that building within the current ordinances and building a
multilevel structure would not be aesthetically pleasing.

Gary Szczepanski asked about it being a multi-story from the plans. It is not; it is a
single story structure in the plans. They don’t have the height clearance to do that.

Jack Coon said that some of the square footage includes wheelchair accessible
doorways which make the footage larger.

Szczepanski stated that this looks to be a large lot, but that they could plan a
different structure that would fit within the ordinances without needed a variance.

Mike Coon stated that, yes, they could do things differently but due to the age and
health condition of their parents, they need to have certain accessibility features
built into the new structure. These include a laundry area, golf cart bay, and other
handicap accessibility features. This also made a multi-story structure out of the
question.

Mr. Richard Coon pointed out that the upper deck could not go out any farther due
to the existing well and stone wall.

Mike Coon mentioned other variances approved in the past 10 years in the same
neighborhood, specifically 1468 Lake Drive, which is 25 feet from the water’s
edge. He also cited other dwellings that are nonconforming. We just want to
remain consistent with the other properties in the surrounding area.

Mrs. Sally Coon stated that Ken Coon had discussed this with the road commission
who said that there was no objection, but Carey said that they are not actually on
the roadway.

Mike Coon also mentioned that the family is growing and it is important to be able
to expand in the future. Engelhardt noted that they do own the dwelling across the

street from the one in question here tonight. Mrs. Sally Coon explained how that
came to be.

Mr. Richard Coon stated that over the many years he has come to this area, he has
seen many changes. And this is one that he would really, really like to see.

Jerry Bridges explained how the ZBA are bound by law to consider 5 conditions
upon which to make their decision. These were set up by the state law in 1977.
Mike Coon states that since then there have been other variances made which do
not conform to those 5 conditions. Gary Szczepanski explained that recent cases
did vary from the guidelines. Jack Coon asked if it is still possible for the board to
make their own decision in spite of the conditions.

So what are the reasons for variances? Bridges explained that certain elements of
the property might make it impossible to conform and then the case would go to
the board for a hearing. The variance allows for irremediable situations to be dealt
with.

Mrs. Sally Coon asked if the way the lake is formed, does that make for a
condition which would allow for a variance. Engelhardt stated that the lot size and
shape will allow for a change in their plans to conform to the ordinances. Support
of neighbors is nice but does not over rule the board’s decision.

Jack Coon asked the board to consider the family’s frustration in relation to the
past decisions that have been made. Mike Coon reiterated that they are attempting
to maintain the aesthetics of the area. He asks the board for their consideration in
this matter. They are pressed for time and want to break ground this spring.

Szczepanski asks: what would it do if you changed the angle of the structure in
relation to the lot? It would not help. They are asking for the same distance from
the lake that the current structure is, but shifting it over to the center of the new lot.
Carey said that no, the deck is included and would make it an additional 12 feet.

Bridges stated that the way they are doing it, they are actually asking for an
additional 17 feet more.

Mr. Richard Coon said he was told that they couldn’t do this due to environmental
reasons. Carey said that this was the previous zoning administrator.

Carey said that he had to turn it down because he cannot allow any buildings to
violate setbacks. This is your appeal to this board and then if denied, you can go to
the circuit court.

Carey, when asked what he expects them to build, said that they have ample
footage to build on without violating setbacks.

Mr. Richard Coon asked if they are on someone else’s property. He was told no.
Then what difference does it make?

Discussion about the square footage clarified that the garage and decks are
included in the calculations which are looked at for setbacks.

Engelhardt further clarified that the full structure with the garage and decks does
exceed the limits. Bridges stated that if the Coons would just redraw the plans they
could build the house within the setbacks allowed.

Mrs. Sally Coon asked about the “one wall” rule. This no longer applies. That law
was done away with.

Mike Coon stated that they know they are out of compliance, but that they are
coming to the board for help, based on past practices.

Jack Coon reinforced that stating that new families have moved in and been
granted variances, while their family who has been here since the 1940’s is being
denied.

Bridges stated that this is the way things are going in the state now except in the
non-zoned townships.

Godau stated that we have more and more ordinances to keep the area from
becoming overcrowded and unappealing.

Carey asked to be recognized by the chair. He sympathizes but asks the family to
consider those owners who have abided by the ordinances in the past and changed
their plans to abide within the laws without recourse to variances.

General discussion about how ordinances came into existence and why.
