Purpose: Chair Godau read the following information: To consider the
application of David and Karen Schankin for a Request for Variance for property
#18-010-720-704-00 located at 4911 Lake Road, Farwell, Michigan. Property
owners request approval to construct an accessory building (26’ x 32’ garage) to be
located in their front yard. This would violate the Lincoln Township Zoning
Ordinance, Section 1502, Paragraph 2, which clearly states, “detached accessory
buildings shall not be erected in any required front yard with the exception of item
eight (8) for waterfront lots listed below.” The notice of this variance was
published in the Clare County Cleaver September 25, 2014, in accordance with the
township zoning ordinances. Notifications were sent to all property owners within
300 feet of the property for the purpose of public comment being made to the
ZBA. The public hearing information was also posted at the township hall and on
the Township’s website in the calendar of events.

Brief Explanation of the Rules: Chair Godau explained that variance requests are
submitted by the persons making the request. The Lincoln Township Board of
Appeals must follow the variance standards created by Michigan Statute. These
provide that the ZBA must determine that a Practical Difficulty exists in order to
grant a variance. These statutes ARE LAW and provide that: 1. There is a
Practical Difficulty; 2. The circumstances are Unique to the Property; 3. There is
Substantial Justice; 4. The issue was Not Self-Created; Minimum Variance
Necessary

Attachment #1

The ZBA should grant a variance from the township's zoning laws only after the
property in question meets stringent tests described in the zoning ordinance. Only the
rarest situations should qualify for relief from the zoning ordinance's requirements
because the zoning regulations are ultimately intended to protect the general health,
safety and welfare of the township. Each time a variance from those regulations is
granted without meeting the prescribed tests, it erodes the integrity of the township's
zoning regulations and their ability to consistently protect all township residents.

Decisions must be based solely on the applicable provisions of the zoning ordinance
and the ability of the applicant's property to qualify for some relief from those
provisions based on the ordinance's standards and the location's physical
characteristics.

Practical Difficulty: Lincoln Township has a set of criteria a person must meet to
be allowed a Variance. An applicant must meet each of the 5, not 4 or 3, all 5
practical difficulties. Michigan appellate court has held that the Zoning Board of
Appeals cannot grant a Variance without substantial evidence of all 5. This is
actually an open and shut case as Mr. Schankin only answered one question and
according to the application he is supposed to answer all five.

Number One, there are practical difficulties of carrying out the strict letter of
the zoning so that in granting the proposed variance the spirit of the zoning
ordinance is observed, public safety is secured and substantial justice is
done.

The Schankin’s have an option, they can move their Drain field and move
the garage back to conform to our ordinance thus there is no practical
difficulty.

If Mr. Schankin were to have answered all the questions, my response would
have been:

Number Two, the condition or situation of the property is unique and not
shared by neighboring properties in the same zone.

The Schankin’s property is pretty much the same as many other properties in
White Birch, no practical difficulty.

Number Three, a variance would not be significantly detrimental to adjacent
property and the surrounding neighborhood.
Not applicable

Number Four, the practical difficulty was not self created.
In this situation it was self created, this is from our Zoning Ordinance of
1977: Detached accessory buildings shall not be erected in any
required front yard. Mr. Schankin bought his property in 2000 and built
his home in 2001. When he built his house Zoning had been in effect for 24
years.

Number Five, Minimum Variance Necessary.

This does not apply as there is ability to build the garage without a variance.
