                     Lincoln Township Zoning Board of Appeals
                                 Annual Meeting
                              Wednesday, April 8, 2015

The meeting was called to order by Chair Godau at 7:00 pm.

Roll Call:
Present – Chair Godau, Vice-Chair Swanson, Bridges, Engelhardt, Smith,
McCaslin, Recording Secretary Sherrod
Absent – Zoning Administrator Carey (excused) Secretary Szczepanski (excused)
Public – 0

Approval of Minutes:
Regular Meeting minutes of March 11, 2015 – It was moved by Bridges and
seconded by Engelhardt to accept the minutes as corrected for time of adjournment
(5:50 pm corrected to read 8:50 pm) . Motion passed unanimously.
General Comments from Chair Godau:
1. Chair Godau presented the Board with a policy concerning the use of social
media by Lincoln Township employees and representatives that he was given by
township treasurer Maggie Carey.
2. Chair Godau also brought up the Lincoln Township ZBA Rules and Procedures
to make sure that everyone on the board has them and has had time to review them.
A discussion concerning the responsibilities of secretary and recording secretary
followed.
Election of Officers:
      a. Bridges made a motion to leave the Chair and Vice-Chair as is, with Tom
Godau as Chair and Marshall Swanson as Vice-Chair. Engelhardt seconded.
Motion passed unanimously.
Zoning Board of Appeals Minutes          2                                4/08/2015


      b. Godau made a motion to nominate Cindy Engelhardt as Secretary,
Bridges seconded. Motion passed unanimously.

New Business:
1. Chair Godau brought up the procedures of how motions are made and acted
upon during hearings and then how different scenarios are handled in the cases of
split votes, multiple motions, the ability to table a request in the case of an
impasse, etc. Bridges and Smith explained that sometimes in the case of an
impasse, the issue will need to be addressed at a future meeting.
2. Future Training Options: Chair Godau presented information on training
sessions available through the township. He asked the board to look over the
information and to get back to him.
3. Schedule of Required Meetings:
The schedule will be set at the next meeting.

Public Comments:
Kim Smith asked as a public attendee: Does the ZBA think they ever will approve
a variance? Bridges and Godau both said that they would not rule out that
possibility. Engelhardt stated that with the requirements to meet the five
conditions it would not be impossible to approve a variance, but it would not be a
simple issue. She stated that this type of problem in the future will be corrected
with the major revisions in the ordinances currently underway. Bridges stated that
the current ordinances are the only thing we have to interpret and we are bound by
those.

Motion to adjourn was made by Bridges, seconded by Swanson. Meeting
adjourned at 8:17 pm by Chair Godau.

Respectfully Submitted,

Secretary Engelhardt
