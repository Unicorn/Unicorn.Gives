                     Lincoln Township Zoning Board of Appeals
                                 Annual Meeting
                             Wednesday, April 13, 2016

The meeting was called to order by Chair Godau at 7:00 pm.

Roll Call:
Present – Board Members: Bridges, Engelhardt, Godau, McCaslin, Swanson,
Staff: Zoning Administrator Carey, Recording Secretary Sherrod
Absent – Kim Smith, Building Inspector Mantei
Public – None

Approval of Minutes:
Regular Meeting minutes of August 12, 2015 – It was moved by Bridges and
seconded by Swanson to accept the minutes as presented. Motion passed
unanimously.

Election of Officers: Bridges made a motion to suspend the rules of election and
retain the slate of offices for the calendar year through April 2017. McCaslin
seconded. Roll call vote called: Bridges: Yes; Engelhardt: Yes; Godau: Yes;
McCaslin: Yes; Swanson: Yes. Motion passed with all in favor.

New Business:
1. Review and approval of ZBA By-laws: Chair Godau referred to the by-law
section in the ZBA notebook mentioned below. He called for a motion to accept
the Zoning Board of Appeals Bylaws and Rules of Procedure as presented.
Bridges made the motion. McCaslin seconded. Motion passed 5 - 0

2. Distribution and review of ZBA notebook: Secretary Engelhardt presented each
member with a notebook containing all the pertinent and helpful materials for the
fulfillment of their duties on the board of appeals.

3. Review of ZBA Variance Requests/Public Meetings 2014-2015: The Board
looked back at the decisions that they have made over the past two years and heard
some of the updates as presented by ZA Carey.

Public Comments: None

Motion to adjourn was made by Bridges, seconded by Swanson. Meeting
adjourned at 7:32 pm by Chair Godau.

Respectfully Submitted,

Secretary Engelhardt
