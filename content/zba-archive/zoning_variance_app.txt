PROCEDURE: as outlined in the Lincoln Township Zoning Ordinance #44, Article 8, Section 8.7 Variances
Subsection C. Including but not limited to:

The applicant shall provide the application and include a plot plan, site plan, elevation drawing or similar drawing
prepared by a registered land surveyor or registered engineer that clearly illustrates property lines, property line bearings
and dimensions, existing buildings and structures, and the proposed improvements to the lot for which the variance is
requested. It shall be the responsibility of the applicant to provide information, plans, testimony, and/or evidence from
which the ZBA may make the required findings, including any information the applicant may choose to submit to
demonstrate conformance with the standards of subsection B. (the seven (7) questions on Pg. 02 of the application.)

Upon receipt of an application, the chairperson of the ZBA shall fix a reasonable time and date for a hearing, taking into
account adequate time for members of the ZBA to review the application, and for proper public notice of the hearing. Per
the Lincoln Township Zoning Ordinance #44, the ZBA shall take action on the request within sixty (60) days of the
applicant’s submittal of a complete application.

Notice of the request must be published in a newspaper of general circulation in the Township no less than 15 days prior
to a hearing. Notice of the request must be sent by first class mail or personal delivery to the owner of the property, the
applicant if different than the owner, and to all persons to whom real property is assessed within 300 feet of the boundary
of the project subject to the request, and all occupants of all structures within 300 feet of the property, regardless of
whether the property or occupant is located in Lincoln Township. Other notices may be sent to organizations or public
utilities or railroads who have made such a request.
