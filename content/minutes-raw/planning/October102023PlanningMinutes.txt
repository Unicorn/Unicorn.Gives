Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Master Plan progress: Wade Trim will be at the November 14 meeting to present the
draft of the master plan.

New Business:
1. Discussion of new ordinance for Hazardous Materials Spill Cost Recovery: This
would be a memo of understanding with the county that in the case of a large spill,
the township will not be responsible for the cost of the clean-up. This will be
further discussed at the November 14 meeting.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, November 14, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by Ostrowski to adjourn the meeting; seconded by McCaslin. Motion
passed with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:30 p.m.

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
