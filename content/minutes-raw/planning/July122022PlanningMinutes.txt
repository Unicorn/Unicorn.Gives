Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2
Several comments were made.

Public Comment: Several comments were made.

Old Business:
1. Continued Master Plan review: Chair Bridges explained how much the township has
changed since the Master Plan was last updated about 20 years ago. Blaisdell has done
some research and Tobin and Bridges have approached Clerk Majewski concerning
getting some professional help with the current update. Blaisdell stated that he could go
into the latest census data and compare it to the two previous censuses to see how the
township population has evolved. He has volunteered to do this comparison and bring
the results back to the commission.

Bridges stated that this will be helpful to project our goals and help the commission be
ready to work with a professional after November. Therefore, this item will be tabled
until the November meeting.

2. Discussion of pending approved ordinance matters concerning the past meeting’s
discussion on the status of the R-2A zoning designation. Bridges stated that the
definitions need to be clarified with the help of the township attorney. And therefore,
she proposed tabling this matter until the next meeting in August.

She asked that White Birch residents would compile their opinions on the status of R-
2A zoning.

Blaisdell read a list of comments and questions that he has received over the past
several weeks concerning the two designations of R-2 and R-2A.

McCaslin made a motion that the discussion about the R-2/R-2A be tabled until the
residents of White Birch have had a chance to compile valid facts and information
concerning the benefits of keeping R-2A, and an opinion from the township attorney
has been received. This period shall be no longer than 60 days. Szczepanski seconded.
Motion carried with all in favor.

New Business:
1. Bridges proposed that the August meeting be cancelled since the two major items
have been tabled until at least September and November. Discussion followed. The
majority of the commission supported this. [NOTE: Several days after the meeting was
adjourned, it became apparent that the August meeting needed to be reinstated, so the
next meeting will be August 9.]
