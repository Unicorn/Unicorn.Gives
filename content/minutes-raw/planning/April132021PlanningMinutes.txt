Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2
Several questions were asked and discussed.

Public Comment: ZA Carey clarified a question that was posed about a tarp shed.

Old Business:
1. Revisit and discuss Rental (STR) Ordinances: McCaslin sent out a draft of a
Harrison STR rental ordinance. Bridges also mentioned that Surrey Township and the
City of Clare have their own STR rental ordinances that we can look at to adapt for
Lincoln’s use. McCaslin stated that it is time to adopt an ordinance for this, but there
will have to be clarity on who will enforce it. This is all provisional on Township
Board approval. Chair Hassberger asked McCaslin to chair the committee; Bridges and
ZA Carey will also be on the committee. McCaslin clarified that this should be generic
to cover both short and long term rentals.

New Business:
1. MAP workshop discussion: Hassberger, McCaslin, and ZA Carey attended.
2. Discuss Master Plan Review and Re-approval: Hassberger will convene a committee
to begin working on this, reviewing and revisiting the old plan.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, May 11, 2021, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by Bridges to adjourn the meeting; seconded by McCaslin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:36 p.m.


Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
