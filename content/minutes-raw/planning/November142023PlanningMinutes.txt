Regular Meeting – Called to order at 7:00 pm by Chair Blaisdell.

Roll Call:
Present – Blaisdell, McCaslin, Ostrowski, Szczepanski, Tobin, Hassberger,
Sherrod
Absent – Trim
Public – 4 in person; 1 via Zoom

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of October 10, 2023. Ostrowski made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented; McCaslin seconded. Motion passed.

Approval of Agenda: Accepted as corrected

Master Plan – “Goals and Objectives Future Land Use Plan & Implementation”:
Adam Young from Wade Trim gave a report on the progress of the Master Plan.
He reviewed the latest draft dated November 13, 2023. General discussion
followed. Next steps are to submit the draft plan to the township board for
approval and then letters will go out to surrounding entities (neighboring
townships, the county, etc.). Then there will be a public hearing. Wade Trim will
be assisting the township with these next steps. Chair Blaisdell will collect
comments from the commission to send to Adam.

One comment was made.

Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Discussion of new ordinance for Hazardous Materials Spill Cost Recovery: ZA
Hassberger completed the template with township specific information to make it ready
for adoption. Tabled until December 12 meeting.

New Business:
1. Special Use Permit request by Stuart Cline to use the former bank building as a
recreational equipment rental (kayaks, canoes, boats, side by sides, golf carts, etc.).
He would also include a boat shop to provide services, repairs, and products that
are otherwise only available elsewhere in the county.

McCaslin had questions about locations for storage and other usage. Her other
concern was about power washing to avoid the spread of invasive species between
lakes.

There will be a public hearing on January 9, 2024, to address the description of this
type of business as it is not included in the zoning. ZA Hassberger will provide a
set of conditions for the business.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, December 12, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Ostrowski. Motion
passed with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:33 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
