Regular Meeting – Tuesday, November 15, 2022, at 7:00 pm. Called to order at
7:00 pm by Chair Bridges.

Roll Call:
Present – Bridges, Blaisdell, McCaslin, Szczepanski, Tobin, Hassberger, Sherrod
Absent –Trim
Public – 5 in person

Public Hearing: Chair Bridges opened the public hearing for the purpose of
considering rezoning from R1 to R2 the properties located in the southwest corner
of Section 16. The properties are located on West Lake George Drive, Lincoln
Township, Harrison, Michigan 48625, and include property numbers from 010-
016-300-03 to 13, 010-298-001-00 to 023-00, 010-760-040-01, and 010-017-100-
01 and 03.

Bridges explained the procedure of addressing the chair, stating name and address
when making public comment.

Public Comment: None

Chair Bridges asked if Mr. Neill knows of how many neighbors are for or against
this change. There was one email received that opposes this rezoning. Discussion
followed concerning the individual properties which will be affected.

McCaslin spoke her concern about the lakeside lots that shouldn’t be R1 to begin
with. She also reiterated her feeling that rezoning for just the Neill property could
be considered spot zoning. She proposes denying the Neill application to rezone
only that property and then look at the whole area to be rezoned from R1 to R2.

Tobin revisited the idea that originally the zoning was done incorrectly and has
always needed to be R2 instead of R1.

Blaisdell agreed that this needs to be corrected for all the properties.

Bridges moved to close the public hearing at 7:15 pm: Szczepanski seconded.
Bridges then opened the regular Planning Commission meeting.

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of October 11, 2022. Blaisdell made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented; McCaslin seconded. Motion passed.

Approval of Agenda: Accepted with no amendments.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
There were several questions asked and answered as well as several comments.

Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
No questions or comments.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2
No questions or comments.

Public Comment: A White Birch board member brought and distributed a letter
from the WB board concerning the R2 & R2-A recreation matter.

Old Business:

1. Continued Master Plan Review: Bridges recommended tabling this again until Tobin
can get more information from Clerk Majewski.

1a. Decision on the Neill application to rezone a single property from R1 to R2:
Blaisdell made a motion to deny the Neill claim to change the properties #010-016-300-
04 and 010-016-300-11 from R1 to R2. Szczepanski seconded. Bridges asked for
discussion. Bridges called for a roll call vote: Bridges: Yes; Szczepanski: Yes;
                    Lincoln Township Is An Equal Opportunity Provider
Planning Commission Minutes                3                              11/15/2022


McCaslin: Yes; Tobin: Yes; Blaisdell: Yes. The motion passed to deny the Neill
application.

Chair Bridges re-opened the public hearing on the issue of rezoning the prior mentioned
properties from R1 to R2 and, hearing no more public comments, she closed the public
hearing.

Szczepanski made a motion to recommend to the board that the properties on W. Lake
George Dr. be changed from R1 to R2. Bridges called for a roll call vote.
Bridges: Yes; Szczepanski: Yes; McCaslin: Yes; Tobin: Yes; Blaisdell: Yes. The
motion passed with all in favor.

The planning commission will write a letter to the township board with this
recommendation. Mr. Neill will now need to re-apply to do a lot split if and when the
board approves the rezoning.

2. Discussion of Pending Approved Ordinance Matters: McCaslin made a motion that
the planning commission recommend to the township board to remove the designation
wording R2-A from the zoning ordinance as it is no longer relevant. Szczepanski
seconded.

Discussion opened: Bridges moved to table the motion until after the required public
hearing. McCaslin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor.

New Business:
1. Blaisdell spoke about a group working on getting a ball field built in Lake George.
He wanted to acknowledge all the work that they did in making this proposal. He feels
that they are doing a good job in organizing not only this but other activities that they
are starting.

2. ZA Hassberger brought up the trend of putting a tiny home on a trailer and how the
ordinance needs to address this. He will work on a definition to add to the ordinance
with the help of Bridges and Blaisdell.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, December 13, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by Bridges to adjourn the meeting; seconded by McCaslin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:19 p.m.
