Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
Tobin commented that the well & septic reports have not been sent for some time.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2
Several comments were made.

Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Continue Master Plan Review: Work is tabled to allow new member Phil Blaisdell to
get up to date. Continue work at the July 12 meeting.

New Business: R2A Designation: McCaslin went through the ordinance and found 17
instances/mentions of R2A. McCaslin feels this designation is no longer needed due to
changes in the ordinance which removes the need for it. This refers to the amount of
time allowed to keep a camper on a lot. Blaisdell asked for clarification, if this action
would remove confusion. McCaslin and Bridges confirmed.

McCaslin stated that there will need to be clarification on what steps need to be taken to
go forward with removing the R2A zoning designation. The township attorney will be
consulted concerning the proper action to take.

McCaslin made a motion that the planning commission begin evaluating if it is feasible
to recommend to the township board to remove the R2A designation from the
ordinance and from the zoning map. Blaisdell seconded. Motion passed with all in
favor.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, July 12, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:55 p.m.


Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
