Regular Meeting – Tuesday, July 13, 2021, at 7:00 pm. Called to order at 7:01
pm by Chair Hassberger.

Roll Call:
Present – Hassberger, McCaslin, Szczepanski, Tobin, Carey, Sherrod
Absent – Bridges, excused
Public – 12

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of June 15, 2021. McCaslin made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented. Tobin seconded. Motion passed.

Approval of Agenda: Approved with no changes.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
ZA Carey also read the following statement:
        “Many of you have read the letter I sent to Vicky. I will stand behind that
100% even in court. Roof is the cover of a building making it a structure. A roof
shall also be construed to include a cover over an outdoor area. A canopy as I see
it is a structure, a lot of people have questioned this, and questioned an umbrella
over a picnic table. I read parts of the Ordinance over and over because you rarely
read them the same. Today I found what I would call a loophole. Our zoning
states anything that exceeds 60 square feet in floor area or eight feet in height is a

Hassberger moved to accept the Zoning Administrator’s report. Tobin seconded.

Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
No questions were asked. Hassberger moved to accept the Building Inspector’s
report. Tobin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See attachment #2
Tobin read full report.

Several questions were asked and discussed.

Old Business:
1. Revisit and discuss Rental Ordinances: McCaslin had sent out a draft copy of the
rental ordinance for everyone to review. She briefly went over some of the changes she
made to the original draft. She would like to present this at a public hearing if the
planning commission members approve of doing so and then pass it on to the township
board of trustees. She emphasized that this is not a short term rental ordinance; it is a
rental ordinance without specifying length of time.

Szczepanski made a motion to present this at a public hearing and go forward with it.
McCaslin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor. The public hearing will be in
August.

2. Discuss plans for Master Plan review and re-approval: Chair Hassberger reported
that this is an ongoing review.

3. Discussion of Blighted Properties: No comments on this topic at this time.

New Business: None

Public Comment: Several comments were made concerning White Birch Lakes
and the township ordinances.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, August 10, 2021, at
7:00 pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:57 p.m.

                                          Other

 In regard to the 10 White Birch complaints I filed, 5 camper and 1 accessory building
 complaints have been resolved. I have allowed 1 extension and I have filed a second
                          complaint on 3 accessory buildings.


I received a complaint of a vicious dog this month. Vicious dog is a dog that kills another
   dog, in this case a pit bull killed a Chihuahua. I have filed a complaint demanding the
   dog be removed from the township according to our ordinance. The County has also
             filed a dangerous dog complaint which will be going to court soon.
                         The following is from Rudi (animal control):
 We ask people to please call in if they see a loose dog. We do need photo’s if the case
  goes to court (usually, unless there are several witnesses), but we still want people to
call ,so A. we can go out and possibly catch the dog running, and B. To give the owners’
  a verbal warning, and C. To start a case file on those people and those dogs. It’s very
frustrating to us and the people that call when they say “this dog is ALWAYS loose” and
  we ask them if they have called and made a complaint? And they say no. We have no
 case then, when something terrible happens, we have no history. Frustrating for all. I’m
 not sure we will win a “Dangerous Dog” case, on this dog, but we are willing to give it a
 try. Usually those are for dogs that show aggression to people, but since this dog killed
 another dog, it’s worth a shot, and it sets us up in case the dog gets loose again. Make
                                sense? Call if we can help. Rudi
