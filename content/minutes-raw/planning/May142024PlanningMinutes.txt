No questions or comments.

Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Update Lincoln Township Zoning Map: Members each received copies of the
current map and a copy of the new, corrected map to examine the changes. Chair
Blaisdell asked everyone to look closely for any discrepancies or errors and bring them
to the next meeting.

2. Update to the Lincoln Township Recreational Master Plan: Tobin handed out copies
of the current recreational plan for review. Chair Blaisdell asked everyone to look it
over and make notes on what changes they think need to be made. McCaslin suggested
that at the next meeting, each member take a section to work on in more detail.

3. Amendment to Ordinance 18.27: This concerns the type of wireless communication
tower being allowed under the ordinance. According to ZA Hassberger, there is an
ambiguity in the language. ZA Hassberger has proposed two versions of an
amendment: one to allow a lattice type tower and the other to explicitly prohibit the
lattice type tower. The new language would be inserted in Section 18.27, E. 3. Tower
Construction, item a. Discussion followed. ZA Hassberger thinks that the ordinance
needs to be changed to allow lattice towers in order to prevent problems in the future.
McCaslin, Ostrowski, and Blaisdell feel that the ordinance should not be changed until
there is a definite need to do so as in an application being filed. The Planning
Commission discussed the issue and decided not to take action to amend the ordinance
at this time.

New Business:
None

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, June, 11, 2024, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:00 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
