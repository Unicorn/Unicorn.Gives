Public Hearing:
Chair Blaisdell opened the hearing at 7:00 pm, and explained the purpose of the
hearing and the procedure to be followed. He also reviewed the process up to this
point.

1. Lincoln Township Master Plan: Chair Blaisdell opened the hearing to public
comments.

McCaslin had a question about the environmental contamination sites concerning
whether the areas had been reviewed recently. Adam Young, a representative from
Wade Trim, said that they will check with EGLE to find out the most current
status.

Her next question was about the property owned by the gas company, specifically
the official designation of Hamlin Field. Adam from Wade Trim replied that it
should remain labeled as recreation even though the gas company owns it with a
lease by the township.

Her last question concerned the source of the pictures in the master plan. Adam
replied that the Wade Trim team members took them when they were in the area.
McCaslin wanted to have some more lake photos included. McCaslin will contact
Dale Majewski and others to get some more pictures which better represent the
lakes in the township.

Tobin asked about making amendments to the Master Plan. Discussion followed. It was
decided to go ahead with the adoption of the plan and then send the final product to the
township board.

Chair Blaisdell then read the resolution to adopt the Master Plan as follows:

                               Resolution of Adoption
                           Lincoln Township Master Plan
           Lincoln Township Planning Commission, Clare County, Michigan

WHEREAS, Lincoln Township has established a Planning Commission under the
Planning Enabling Act, Public Act 33 of 2008, as amended; and,

WHEREAS, The Lincoln Township Planning Commission is required by Section 31 of
said Act to prepare and adopt a master plan as a guide for the physical development of
the township; and,

WHEREAS, A proposed master plan was prepared and was approved for distribution
by the Township Board of Lincoln Township and was subsequently submitted for review
in accordance with Section 41 of said Act; and,

WHEREAS, The plan was presented to the public at a hearing on March 12, 2024,
before the Planning Commission, with notice of the hearing being provided in
accordance with Section 43 of said Act; and,

WHEREAS, The Township Planning Commission has determined that the plan is
appropriate for the future development of the township;

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT, The Lincoln Township Planning
Commission does hereby adopt the Lincoln Township Master Plan.

Ostrowski made the motion to adopt the resolution; Tobin seconded.

Roll Call vote: Blaisdell: Yes; McCaslin: Yes; Ostrowski: Yes; Tobin: Yes; motion
passed with all in favor, 4 Yes; 0 No.

Chair Blaisdell thanked Adam and Wade Trim for all their work and for making the
process easy for the commission. Tobin also made a positive comment.

New Business:

1. Discussion to update Lincoln Township Zoning Map: Chair Blaisdell explained about
the next steps in getting the zoning maps updated. There will need to be some careful
review of recent changes and other items to make sure the new maps are accurate.
Blaisdell and Tobin will check with Clerk Majewski to get a large current map printed
for review purposes.

2. Chair Blaisdell reopened the discussion of the drainage ditch issue at Silver Lake in
order to help the concerned parties plan their next steps in determining who to contact.
The problem is that the road commission denies it is in their authority and the township
doesn’t deal with drains. Tobin will get in touch with the drain commissioner to find
out more. ZA Hassberger found it on the original plat map showing drain easement of
15 feet. More discussion followed trying to find out who would have answers and can
authorize the work on cleaning the drain.

3. ZA Hassberger handed out information concerning the construction of a cellular
tower which brings up some questions about Zoning Ordinance 44 as to the type of
tower that is allowed under the ordinance. He is asking for direction from the Planning
Commission and the ZBA. The Planning Commission will do research to see how to
resolve this issue. Chair Blaisdell will have this on the agenda for April.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, April 9, 2024, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
