Old Business:

1. Continued Master Plan Review: Tobin advised that this will be addressed after the
November election. Tabled until the November 15 meeting.

2. Discussion of Pending Approved Ordinance Matters including R-2A and the
rezoning hearing scheduled for November 15. Chair Bridges read the public notice for
the hearing that will be published in the Clare County Cleaver and sent out to affected
property owners. McCaslin also explained her research into this matter.

Chair Bridges explained that she spoke to the township attorney, Tara Hovey, about the
issue around R-2 and R-2A. Ms. Hovey recommended that no change be made at this
time due to the expense of changing the ordinance for just this one issue and that any
revision should wait until other changes are made to the ordinance.

McCaslin also explained that in the Master Plan White Birch Lakes is identified several
times as a residential community, not recreational. While in the Recreation Plan only
the campground at White Birch Lakes is identified as recreational.

McCaslin proposed scheduling another public hearing to address both the R-2A issue
and to correct the current zoning maps to reflect some of the changes made recently and
in the near future.

Blaisdell made a comment about concerns that have been raised about changing the R-
2A designation. He recommends taking care of the change now along with the rest of
the zoning map changes to avoid confusion in the future since there is no significant
difference between R-2 and R-2A.

One public comment was made concerning the expense of changing zoning maps and
ordinances before the Master Plan is finished.

New Business: None

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, November 15, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:12 p.m.
