Regular Meeting – Called to order at 7:00 pm by Chair Blaisdell.

Public Hearing: Consideration of granting a Special Use Permit to Todd Bringold
for the property at 525 W. Arthur Rd. currently zoned as General Commercial
which requires the permit to construct mini storage units.
Chair Blaisdell asked Mr. Bringold if he had anything to add to his previous
presentation. He did not and Chair Blaisdell, hearing no other public comment,
closed the public hearing to move on to the regular meeting.

Roll Call:
Present – Blaisdell, Szczepanski, Tobin, Hassberger, Sherrod
Absent – Ostrowski, excused, McCaslin, excused, Trim
Public – 3 in person; 0 via Zoom

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of August 15, 2023. Blaisdell made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented; Szczepanski seconded. Motion
passed.

Approval of Agenda: Accepted with one addition.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
One question was asked and answered.

Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
No questions.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2
One comment was made.

Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Property at 525 W. Arthur Rd. Planning Commission discussion and decision.
Chair Blaisdell asked for comments. ZA Hassberger stated that the plans are in
compliance, and he sees no reason not to grant the permit.
Szczepanski made a motion to grant the special use permit for construction of mini
storage units at 525 W. Arthur Rd. Tobin seconded. No further discussion.
Motion passed with all in favor.

2. Master Plan progress: Chair Blaisdell reviewed the Master Plan Visioning
Workshop held on August 22 and said that it had been fairly successful.

New Business:
1. Upcoming in next meeting: discussion of Hazardous Materials Cost Recovery.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, October 10, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by Szczepanski to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:37 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
