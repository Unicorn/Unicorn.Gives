Regular Meeting – Tuesday, December 13, 2022, at 7:00 pm. Called to order at
7:00 pm by Chair Bridges.

Roll Call:
Present – Bridges, Blaisdell, McCaslin, Szczepanski, Tobin, Hassberger, Sherrod
Absent –Trim
Public – 5 in person

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of November 15, 2022. Szczepanski
made a motion to approve the minutes as presented; Tobin seconded. Motion
passed.

Approval of Agenda: Accepted with an addition to old business and the election
of officers.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
Two questions were asked and answered.

Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
No questions or comments.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2

One question was asked.

Public Comment: One comment was made concerning the discussion of
ordinance enforcement.

Old Business:

1. Continued Master Plan Review: The Township has hired Wade-Trim, Inc. to help
with updating the Master Plan.
2. Discussion of public hearing about consolidating R2 and R2A: After reviewing the
minutes from November, McCaslin suggested holding the public hearing and then
sending the recommendation to the board for them to decide what to do.
      The public hearing for this will be held at the February 14 planning meeting and
a public notice will be published in the Clare County Cleaver and sent to the White
Birch Lakes newsletter.

New Business:
None

Election of Officers:
Bridges nominated Blaisdell as Chair. He accepted.
Tobin nominated Bridges as Vice Chair. She accepted.
Bridges nominated Szczepanski as Secretary. He accepted.

Szczepanski made a motion to vote to accept the nominations and vote on them as
one slate; McCaslin seconded. Motion carried with all in favor of the slate of new
officers.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, January 10, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:41 p.m.


Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
