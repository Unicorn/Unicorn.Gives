Public Hearings:
Chair Blaisdell opened the hearings at 7:00 pm, and explained the procedure to be
followed. The commission will address both issues in Old Business.

1. Stuart Cline “Old Bank Building” Special Use Permit at 440 Lake George St.:

McCaslin began by asking the percentage of the business between rental and retail.
Mr. Cline answered to her satisfaction that it will be about 50-50 between the two.
Tobin asked about the disposal of the water from power washing watercraft. He
replied that he has several options to prevent the water from going directly into the
lake. A general discussion followed concerning invasive species and preventing
their spread in lakes.

Mr. Cline will limit the use of boats to the lake in the rental contract and will be
able to use GPS to monitor the location of the boat.

Chair Blaisdell closed that public hearing.

2. New ordinance for Hazardous Material Spill Cost Recovery:

Chair Blaisdell then opened the next public hearing to consider the new ordinance
for hazardous material spill. He explained the reason for this ordinance, using the

2. Stuart Cline SUP: McCaslin had several comments concerning the change in
wording of the application. She stated that if his business is going to be mostly retail,
he does not need the SUP. Her other concern is about storage and how much will be
stored.

Mr. Cline explained his purpose of storage as being temporary storage between
rentals. There will be no customer storage. He will use a different site if storage
needs grow past the capacity of this property.

McCaslin asked about the use of the existing sign, and Mr. Cline confirmed his
intention to do so with no changes in size.

Chair Blaisdell explained that Mr. Cline can come back and apply for a SUP if his
storage becomes too large for this current property.

The decision was made that no Special Use Permit is necessary after all.

3. New ordinance for Hazardous Materials Spill Cost Recovery:
McCaslin made the motion to recommend to the township board the Hazardous
Materials Spill Cost Recovery Ordinance for approval. Tobin seconded. Motion
passed with all in favor.

New Business: None

pm.
