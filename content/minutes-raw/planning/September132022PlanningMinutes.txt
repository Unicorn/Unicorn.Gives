Regular Meeting – Tuesday, September 13, 2022, at 7:01 pm. Called to order at
7:00 pm by Chair Bridges.

Roll Call:
Present – Bridges, Blaisdell, McCaslin, , Szczepanski, Tobin, Hassberger, Sherrod
Absent –Trim
Public – 15 in person

Public Hearing: Chair Bridges opened the public hearing for the purpose of
hearing the request from the Neills for rezoning from R1 to R2. Bridges explained
the procedure of addressing the chair, stating name and address when making
public comment.

Tara Hovey, attorney for the Neills, explained that the property purchased was
zoned R2 at the time of purchase, but that changed some time since 1976. They
are asking to have it rezoned back to R2 as prior to 1976 so that they can build on
the lot in question. They want to purchase a part of 300-011 across the street from
their own property at 300-04 to build a garage on it. In order for this to be
possible, they need the change in zoning to have enough property size to meet
setbacks. Also, this would make the property in question contiguous so they don’t
need to build a residence that they don’t need.

McCaslin, Blaisdell, and Bridges asked clarifying questions.

Currently they have a purchase agreement in answer to Tobin’s question. They
explained that they don’t own it yet, but Hovey explained that with a purchase
agreement, they have a vested interest in the matter of rezoning.

Mr. Neill went on to explain what the original owner had planned in developing
this property.

Szczepanski asked if the current piece would be buildable with the change in
rezoning. Hovey answered in the affirmative.

Bridges clarified that they don’t want to buy the whole lot as that would put the
owner out of compliance with his own pole building. Bridges went on to ask if it
was changed to R2, they could purchase the whole acre if they wanted to. The
answer was yes, but they had run into problems with other factors.

The property in question, 300-11, needs to be entirely zoned as R2 in order to be
used in this manner. The Neills currently own 300-04.

Further discussion continued to make sure the commissioners understood the exact
location, shape, and size of the properties in question and exactly what the Neills
are asking for.

McCaslin speculated that perhaps these properties were mismarked on the map
during the changes when the ordinance was redone in 2017. She also voiced her
opinion that possibly the entire section of R1 properties should be rezoned to R2.
She wants to go back to check if the current zoning map is incorrect.

McCaslin asked if the Neills could wait until more information can be found on
when this zoning happened. Blaisdell said that he is in favor of consistency and
that the whole thing should be rezoned to R2 rather than have spot zoning which
looks bad. Szczepanski agreed.

Jim Firlit spoke; he owns across the street and said that he and other property
owners nearby are in favor of this change, 298-11, 12, & 13. He also wrote an
email about this.

Bridges asked if the commissioners want to do more research on this matter before
making a decision.

Veronica Jamieson asked about any negative input via mail/email. She also lives
on West Lake George.

Tobin stated that the consensus is that this zoning is wrong and needs to be
addressed.

Bridges explained the procedure going forward. McCaslin agreed to search out
and study the old zoning maps to see where the possible mistake was.

The decision was made to schedule another public hearing to change the whole
portion of West Lake George St. from R1 to R2 to be held at the November
planning meeting.

Bridges closed the public hearing at 7:48 pm. and reopened the regular Planning
Commission meeting.
