Regular Meeting – Tuesday, October 12, 2021, at 7:00 pm. Called to order at
7:00 pm by Chair Hassberger.

Roll Call:
Present – Hassberger, McCaslin, Szczepanski, Tobin, Carey, Sherrod
Absent – Bridges, Trim
Public – 6

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of September 14, 2021. Minutes accepted
as amended under New Business, Item 1, adding the words, “regarding accessory
buildings” to the end of the second sentence.

Approval of Agenda: Approved with no changes.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
There were several questions and comments.

Building Inspector’s Report: No report at this time.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See attachment #2
Tobin read full report. Two questions and comments were made.

Public Hearing: Proposed Rental Ordinance

Chair Hassberger read the opening statement of purpose for the proposed
ordinance draft. There were no questions or discussion.

McCaslin made a motion to close the public hearing on the proposed rental
ordinance. Tobin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor.

Public Comment: Several questions and comments were made.

Old Business:
1. Proposed Rental Ordinance: McCaslin explained that this ordinance will cover
rentals of all kinds. There was a short discussion.

McCaslin made a motion that the Planning Commission makes a written
recommendation that the draft Township Rental Property ordinance be reviewed by
township board and that revisions will be sent back to the Planning Commission. Tobin
seconded. The motion passed with all in favor.

2. Master Plan Review: Chair Hassberger reported that he is still working on the
revisions and will report his progress at the next meeting in November.

New Business: None

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, November 9, 2021, at
7:00 pm.

Motion by Hassberger to adjourn the meeting; seconded by McCaslin. Motion
passed with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:32 p.m.


Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
