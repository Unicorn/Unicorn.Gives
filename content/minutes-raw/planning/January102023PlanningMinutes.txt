Public Comment: One question was asked about the Planning Commission’s
involvement and responsibility in relation to the Sparks Grant. Chair Blaisdell
answered and other commission members filled in more details. They all basically
said that the Planning Commission is not responsible for decisions about parks or
anything to do with grants.

Old Business:
1. Updating Master Plan progress: Chair Blaisdell shared with the group the
memorandum from Wade Trim which laid out the plan for updating the master plan.
This included step by step actions and a timeline for each step. This also included
workshops for the planning commission and other township officials in the planning
process. Work will begin in January.

2. R-2A – R-2 Zoning Map Districts Amendment Public Hearing Notice: Chair
Blaisdell read the public notice that will be published in the January 26, 2023, issue of
the Clare County Cleaver. ZA Hassberger made a correction in the public notice
concerning the description of the location of the affected area.

New Business:
1. Zoom discussion: Chair Blaisdell spoke about Zoom being available at all future
planning meetings. ZA Hassberger is prepared for it and will run the Zoom portion of
the meetings. Chair Blaisdell asked about what happens if there is a technical issue and
Zoom is not available, and will that affect the meeting itself and cause it to be
adjourned. ZA Hassberger explained that the Zoom portion is only for the convenience
of spectators and is not required for the holding of the meeting. ZA Hassberger
explained that there is a back-up plan in the case of technical difficulties. Chair
Blaisdell emphasized that he is in favor of having this available for everyone.

Tobin made the motion to begin using Zoom at all Planning Commission meetings
starting in February. Bridges seconded. Motion carried.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, February 14, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by Bridges to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:40 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Deb Sherrod, Recording Secretary
