Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Updating Master Plan progress: Chair Blaisdell reported that 178 surveys have been
returned during May according to Wade Trim. There was discussion about how to
better publicize that the survey is available.

New Business:
1. Presentation: The purchaser of the property at 94 W. Arthur Rd., Greg
Ridenour, gave a presentation on his proposal to build frost-free, climate controlled
storage units on the property. It is currently zoned as C-1(Local Commercial), but
the current zoning does not allow for the type of business he is proposing. There
are also deed restrictions on the property which limit the future uses of the
property. He is asking the Planning Commission to consider a change in zoning
and then provide him with a letter stating that his project would meet with zoning
and be allowed as planned. This letter would help him with final approval of the
sale.

Discussion about the deed restrictions, setbacks, etc. followed as the Planning
Commission members worked to get a clearer idea of the project.

McCaslin asked for clarification on the current zone, C-1 (Local Commercial). ZA
Hassberger explained that the change can be done either by text definition or by
changing to C-3. Another option might be to issue a conditional special use permit
which possibly could be done in a timelier manner.

ZA Hassberger suggested that Mr. Ridenour should bring his site plan to the July
Planning Commission meeting at which time the commission will give him a letter
that will indicate to him and the seller that the commission is looking into the
different options for making this type of business allowable.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, July 11, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by Ostrowski to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Szczepanski. Motion
passed with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:11 p.m.
