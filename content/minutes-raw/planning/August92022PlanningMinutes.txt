Old Business:

1. Continuance of the R2-A Discussion:
Chair Bridges made a statement of clarification concerning the motion made about the
discussion of R2-A. The motion in July tabled the discussion of the viability of R2-A
for no longer than 60 days. Therefore the topic is on the agenda for tonight’s
rescheduled August meeting.

Bridges made a motion to table the discussion about R2-A until the September meeting;
Blaisdell seconded. Motion passed.

2. Continued Master Plan Review: Clerk Majewski is waiting for responses from
contacts she has made for hiring someone to work on this project.

3. Discussion of Pending Approved Ordinance Matters Regarding Recreation: Chair
removed this item from the agenda.

4. Zoom meetings for the Planning Commission: Bridges explained that there is no
requirement for offering a Zoom meeting component to in-person meetings. Discussion
followed. A suggestion was made that there be a dedicated laptop to make this happen.

New Business: Bridges made a motion to accept the file distributed by ZA Hassberger
for the Public Hearing on rezoning which is scheduled for the September 13 meeting.
McCaslin seconded. Motion passed.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, September 13, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by Szczepanski to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:16 p.m.


Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
