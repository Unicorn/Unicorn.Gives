Roll Call:
Present – Bridges, McCaslin, Szczepanski, Tobin, Sherrod
Absent – Carey, Hassberger, excused
Public – 4

Public Hearing: Proposed Ordinance Amendment
This is about an amendment concerning multi frontage lots. This does not concern
a particular lot, but a generic change which would affect several properties.

McCaslin referred to Article 9 about the procedures required for requesting
ordinance amendments. She further went on that there has to be reason or cause to
amend the ordinance.

Vice Chair Bridges closed the public hearing.

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of May 11, 2021. Szczepanski made a
motion to approve the minutes as corrected to reflect who seconded the motion to
approve the Building Inspector’s report. McCaslin seconded. Motion passed.

Approval of Agenda: Approved with no changes.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
There was a short discussion about lot divisions.

Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
No questions were asked.

Ordinance Enforcement Report:
Tobin read full report.
Several questions were asked and discussed.

Old Business:
1. Revisit and discuss Rental Ordinances: McCaslin reported that the state is still
working on passing two bills about rentals. She wants the Planning Commission to go
ahead with our own local rental ordinance. McCaslin will present a document for the
July 13 meeting.

2. Discuss plans for Master Plan review and re-approval: Vice Chair Bridges tabled this
until the next meeting due to absences.

3. Discussion of Blighted Properties: Tabled to the next meeting.

4. Discussion of Article 9: McCaslin reiterated that procedures need to be followed as
laid out in this Article when amendments are being proposed. The form needs to be
corrected and posted on the township’s webpage. Bridges will pursue this with the
board and proceed; she asked Tobin to present the corrected form to the township board
for final approval.

Bridges made a motion for all individuals, including township officials, who are
requesting an amendment to the ordinance follow the procedure that is found in Article
9. Szczepanski seconded the motion. Motion passed with all in favor.

5. Proposed Ordinance Revision: Article 21 – Lots with triple frontage:
Tobin stated that if this revision is not made, it will make a certain property
unbuildable. Bridges said that he should take his problem to the ZBA for a variance.
McCaslin argued for the other property owners who are in similar situations and that
there is no consistency in this matter.

McCaslin made a motion to deny Roger Carey’s application to amend the ordinance
concerning multi-frontage lots with triple frontage to the Board due to lack of
information and/or cause. Szczepanski seconded. Motion passed with all in favor.
