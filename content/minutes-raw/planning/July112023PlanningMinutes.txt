Old Business:
1. Updating Master Plan progress: Wade Trim said that 303 surveys had been done
about a week before the end date of June 30. Wade Trim is asking to hold a
workshop/meeting with property owners in August. The meeting will be held 6 to 8 pm
at the township hall.

New Business:
1. Chair Blaisdell reported that we have received an application for a Special Use
Permit from Greg Ridenour for the property at 94 W. Arthur Road, Lake George,
Michigan. Several points were discussed concerning the timing for the approval of
the permit and the possibility of being able to finalize the purchase. The distance
of the railway right-of-way needs to be determined before the hearing. Chair
Blaisdell made it clear to Mr. Ridenour that he needs to present a more detailed site
plan as soon as possible. McCaslin read the ordinance requirements for the site
plan. Blaisdell made a motion to hold a public hearing for a Special Use Permit for
the property 94 W. Arthur Road, Tuesday, August 15, 2023; McCaslin seconded.
Motion passed.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, August 15, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by Blaisdell to adjourn the meeting; seconded by McCaslin. Motion
passed with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:00 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
