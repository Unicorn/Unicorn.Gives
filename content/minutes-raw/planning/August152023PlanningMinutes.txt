Regular Meeting – Called to order at 7:00 pm by Vice-Chair Ostrowski.

Public Hearing: Consideration of granting a Special Use Permit for the property at
94 W. Arthur Rd. currently zoned as Local Commercial. McCaslin made the
motion to open the public hearing; Tobin seconded. Motion passed with all in
favor.

Mr. Ridenour began by explaining his project. Acting Chair Ostrowski reviewed
the information that has been presented and then asked if the public had any
comments or questions. McCaslin made the motion to close the public hearing;
Tobin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor.

Roll Call:
Present – McCaslin, Ostrowski, Szczepanski, Tobin, Hassberger, Sherrod
Absent – Blaisdell, excused, Trim
Public – 2 in person; 0 via Zoom

Approval of Minutes:
Approval of the regular meeting minutes of July 11, 2023. Ostrowski made a
motion to approve the minutes as presented; Tobin seconded. Motion passed.

Approval of Agenda: Accepted with one addition to New Business.

Zoning Administrator’s Report: See Attachment #1
One question was asked and answered.

Building Inspector’s Report: See Attachment #3
No questions.

Ordinance Enforcement Report: See Attachment #2
Several comments were made.

Public Comment: Todd Bringold introduced himself and asked about the process
for public hearings.

Old Business:
1. Property at 94 W. Arthur Rd. Planning Commission discussion and decision. Acting
Chair Ostrowski asked for Mr. Ridenour to explain his detailed plans for this project.

Ostrowski asked a clarifying question about how many buildings there will be; Mr.
Ridenour said that he will start with one and then add another building later. Both these
buildings are on the site plans. He is working hard to make sure that lighting does not
bother the neighboring properties.

There was more discussion concerning the back setbacks which McCaslin felt were not
to the zoning ordinance requirement of 25 feet. After much discussion, Mr. Ridenour
agreed to comply with the 25 foot setback by adjusting his building’s location.

Szczepanski made a motion to approve Mr. Ridenour’s request for a special use permit
for the property at 94 W. Arthur with all setbacks for C-1 zoning to be met as well as
the provisions set forth in the draft copy of the conditions for the Private/Commercial
Storage Complex as set out below. McCaslin seconded. Motion passed with four votes
in favor and none opposed.

Draft Copy of conditions to be complied with:

Section 18.XX Private/Commercial Storage Complex
       A. Compliance with Table 3-4: See Table 3-4 for standards pertaining to minimum lot
          area, width, frontage, and yard setbacks and maximum building height and lot
          coverage.
       B. Additional Standards and Requirements
          1. Individual Storage Units shall contain a minimum of 360 square feet.

         2. Individual Storage Units will be subject to a MINIMUM 6 (six) month lease.
            Month to month rentals are prohibited, except in the case of extensions of the
            original lease.
         3. No storage of hazardous, toxic, or explosive materials shall be permitted at the
            Complex. The signed lease shall contain this prohibition.
         4. No retail, wholesale, fabrication, manufacturing, or service facilities shall be
            conducted within the units in any Storage unit or within the Complex.
         5. All storage shall be within the enclosed building(s). No outdoor storage shall be
            allowed within the Complex.
         6. Any and all buildings will comply with Clare County and State of Michigan
            Building Codes pertaining to Pole Type Building Construction.
         7. There shall be a minimum of thirty-five(35) feet between buildings within the
            Complex for driveway, parking and fire lane purposes.
         8. The owner of The Complex will be responsible for the adherence to the above
            restrictions by any lessee, and will be responsible for any fines levied for the
            violation thereof.



2. Updating Master Plan progress: The date for the workshop has been set for
Tuesday, August 22, from 6:00 pm to 8:00 pm.

New Business:
1. Todd Bringold has a request to enlarge his storage facility at 525 W. Arthur. ZA
Hassberger explained what he has received for the expansion of an existing storage
building. The property is zoned C-2 which allows ministorage with a special use
permit. A public hearing will be held on September 12 prior to the regular
meeting. Ostrowski made the motion to hold a public hearing for Todd Bringold’s
expansion of his storage building. Szczepanski seconded. Motion passed with all
in favor.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, September 12, at 7:00
pm.

Motion by Tobin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Szczepanski. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:59 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
