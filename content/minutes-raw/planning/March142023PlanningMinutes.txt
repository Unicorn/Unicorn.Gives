Public Comment: One comment was made.

Old Business:
1. Updating Master Plan progress: Brad Doane and Arthur Mullen, Wade Trim
representatives

Arthur began by explaining the preliminary process to begin working on the Master
Plan by contacting townships and utility companies surrounding Lincoln Township. He
also explained how the latest census will be used. They will provide the background
information on which the Planning Commission will build the rest of the plan with the
assistance of Wade Trim.

He went on to explain how the survey will be done and that deciding on the timing for
this survey will be important, given that the population is seasonal.

He finished by highlighting the importance of making sure the Master Plan and the
Zoning Ordinance are tightly connected. The Master Plan is a vision of where we want
to go and is painted with a broad brush as in contrast to the detailed nature of the
Zoning Ordinance.

They will help with the final steps for review and final adoption.

He then reviewed the plan, section by section and made note of changes in laws since
the current plan was adopted as well as places that will need to be updated and filled
out.

Brad then began his presentation with a more detailed focus on Chapter 3:
Socioeconomic Profile, Figures and Tables. He explained the changes in population in
the township and the county and went on to highlight the possible impact of the types of
housing available in the township.

He continued on to review the maps in Chapter 4. Some corrections to text and maps
were pointed out for them to edit.

Arthur then began to speak about the survey. McCaslin gave them the results of the
survey that the previous Master Plan committee used several years ago. Brad continued
by going through the survey in more detail.

Tobin mentioned that we have special assessments to include services from the sheriff’s
office, trash pick-up, and recycling.

Next step is for the Planning Commission to examine these documents and
communicate with Wade Trim through Phil Blaisdell by April 15.

New Business:
     a. Election of Vice Chair: Tobin nominated Jim Ostrowski to be vice chair;
McCaslin seconded. All voted in favor.

       b. John M. & Elvira M. McDonagh Zoning Amendment Petition: ZA Hassberger
explained the petition is to change the zoning on two parcels on Bringold from R-MF
(multifamily) to R-1 (single family). Blaisdell explained that there are two ways to do
this: Rezone the properties or change the ordinance to make it so the R-MF zone can
also be used for single family dwelling. Ostrowski said that it might be better to correct
the zoning. A public hearing will be scheduled for the May 9 Planning Commission
meeting to address the matter of rezoning these two properties from R-MF to R-1.

       c. Bringold Zoning Application and Site Plan: ZA Hassberger gave an
explanation of the Zoning Permit and preliminary site plan to allow for a private
campground. Discussion followed about the requirements for this permit. The
preliminary drawing does not meet the zoning requirements.
       Ostrowski made a motion to reject the preliminary plan based on the information
in front of the Planning Commission. Tobin seconded. Motion passed with all in favor.


Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, April 11, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by Ostrowski to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Tobin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 8:37 p.m.
