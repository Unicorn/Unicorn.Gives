                                  2020 Yearly Zoning Report
 In 2020 I issued or denied 74 zoning permits. I issued 30 accessory building permits, 5
new residence permits, 11 deck permits, 17 addition permits, 2 sign permits and 9 fence
    permits. I issued a total of 47 complaints. I issued 3 violations of Ordinance 32, 38
violations of our Zoning Ordinance, 5 animal violations and 1 violation of Ordinance 38. I
    was involved in 2 Variance applications. I researched and documented at least 33
    zoning issues. I documented over 44 building permits. I was in court 4 times. Plus
                   numerous inspections and undocumented researches.
