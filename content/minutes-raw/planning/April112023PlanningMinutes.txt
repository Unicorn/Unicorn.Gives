Public Comment: One question came up about a property on Bungo Trail. ZA
Hassberger answered the question.

Old Business:
1. Updating Master Plan progress: Chair Blaisdell asked if there were any questions or
comments about the information given out at the last meeting by representatives of
Wade Trim. Blaisdell said that the workshops for this project will begin in May, but no
firm schedule has been set. McCaslin asked a question about the Bertha Lake Park and
its ownership. Blaisdell will verify before the Master Plan is updated. Tobin will also
verify the ownership of all three of the parks, Bertha Lake, Shingle Lake, and Silver
Lake.

2. Update: John M. & Elvira M. McDonagh Zoning Amendment Petition: There will be
a public hearing at the May Planning meeting to make the requested zoning change
from R-MF to R-1.

New Business: None



Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, May 9, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by McCaslin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Ostrowski. Motion
passed with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:37 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
