Old Business:
1. Proposed Rental Ordinance: McCaslin addressed the fact that the public hearing
scheduled for the August meeting was not held and voiced her disappointment that it

                                     Ordinance Complaints

   1. 8/3/21 received a complaint of too many campers and accessory buildings, no
      permanent structure on lot 47 property number 010-660-047-00
   2. 8-9/21 filed second and final complaint of Recreational Vehicle in violation of
      setbacks at 2955 Ojibway. Resolved

After considerable research regarding two campers on one lot, I’ve found that very few
Zoning Ordinances in Michigan allow camping on residential lots and even fewer allow
more than one camper.

I’ve heard how White Birch is a ‘Recreation Residential District’. Our ordinance states
that “In the case of the R-2A District, provide opportunities for single-family and two-
family residential development patterns and lifestyles of a similar character to that of the
R-2 District except for an emphasis on recreation-based neighborhoods with
recreational camping opportunities and the appropriate maintenance and operation
thereof.” As for Camping Opportunities, White Birch has a campground. In all my
research I only found two other Zoning Ordinances with Recreational Residential
Districts and neither one of them allow camping on lots.

As for the people’s response to changing the Zoning from one to two RVs, I have 71 in
favor of leaving zoning at one RV and 26 people in favor of changing to more than one.
