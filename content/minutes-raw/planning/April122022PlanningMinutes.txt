Public Comment: None

Old Business:
1. Master Plan Review: Chair Hassberger began by discussing whether the master plan
needs to be started from scratch or by revising the current one. Everyone agreed that
there is no need to start from scratch. They reviewed what was done most recently in
2017 and McCaslin explained how the commission then could not complete their work
due to the inability to finalize the document in a professional-looking manner. This
would include retyping and formatting the new plan, including color tables, charts,
maps, images, binding, etc.

Bridges asked about the existence of a Parks and Recreation Plan. This will be visited
after the Master Plan is updated and completed.

Bridges suggested finding out if there are college students that could work on this
project. McCaslin said that MSU might prove to be a good source.

There was a discussion of the survey done in 2017-18. They decided it would not be
worthwhile to do another one.

It was decided that Tobin will make copies of the Master Plan, including notes and
revisions from 2017 and distribute them to the members next week. Each member will
review the plan and come to the May meeting ready to make revisions. Chair
Hassberger will have statistics up to date

McCaslin reminded the commission that all utilities and adjacent townships must be
informed that the Master Plan is being revised.

New Business: None

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, May 10, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by Bridges to adjourn the meeting; seconded by McCaslin. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:50 p.m.
