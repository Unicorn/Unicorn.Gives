Public Hearing: Rezoning Properties 010-008-100-04 & 010-008-100-02 (John &
Elvira McDonagh and Mary Lulham). The current zoning is Multiple Family (R-
MF) and the petitioners are requesting to have both properties rezoned to R-1
(single family).

Chair Blaisdell explained how public comment will be done and then opened
public comment.

Public Comment:
Joyce Martin spoke in favor of this change. One favorable correspondence was
received as well.

Ostrowski made a motion to close public hearing; Tobin seconded. Motion carried
with all in favor.

Old Business:
1. Rezoning: Properties 010-008-100-04 & 010-008-100-02 from Multi-family to R-1.

New Business: Chair Blaisdell talked about a person who is interested in purchasing
the vacant land behind the Dollar General for the purpose of elaborate storage units
which could be repurposed for another use in the future. The property is currently
zoned as Local Commercial (C-1) which would prohibit this kind of storage units.

ZA Hassberger has written a draft proposal to amend the C-1 definition to allow for
such units.

All four members present agreed that ZA Hassberger can tell the interested person that
he can go forward with his plans. Tobin will inform the township board about this
tentative proposal.

Announcements: Next Regular Meeting will be Tuesday, June 13, at 7:00 pm.

Motion by Tobin to adjourn the meeting; seconded by Ostrowski. Motion passed
with all in favor. Meeting adjourned at 7:51 p.m.

Respectfully Submitted,

Gary Szczepanski, Secretary
