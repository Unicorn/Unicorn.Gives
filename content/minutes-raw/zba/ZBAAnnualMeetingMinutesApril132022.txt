                                 Annual Meeting
                             Wednesday, April 13, 2022


The meeting was called to order by Chair McCaslin at 7:00 pm.

Roll Call:
Present – Board Members: Bridges, Keller, Lease, McCaslin,
Staff: Recording Secretary Sherrod
Absent – Swanson, Zoning Administrator Carey, excused
Public - 0

Approval of Minutes:
Regular Meeting minutes of May 12, 2021 – Bridges moved to accept the minutes
as written; Lease seconded. Motion carried with all in favor.

Review of 2021 Variance Determinations: Pitock Hearing, January 13, 2021

Review of ZBA Bylaws: Chair McCaslin went over some points for the board to
look at in particular as well as becoming familiar with the whole document.

Training: Chair McCaslin explained that training is required in the bylaws and
that she encourages all members to take as much training as possible as they are
