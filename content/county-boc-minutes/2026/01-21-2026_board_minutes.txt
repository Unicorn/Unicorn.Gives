ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps informed the Commissioners that there have been no major
legislative changes at the State or Federal levels since the December 17th meeting. A
significant amount of time has been dedicated to the process of the Annual County
Audit and it is scheduled to be completed soon. The County has received five bids as
part of the Part 115 Materials Management Planning requirements. A contractor will
be selected by mid-February.

Administrator Phelps stated that she met with the Department heads to distribute
updated approved policies and to discuss the Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 budget process.
She has been working with Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger to implement the new
chart of accounts. Several grant opportunities are her current focus including ones for
homeowner rehabilitation and preservation and protection of county records.

Lastly, Administrator Phelps reported that at this point in the fiscal year the county
budget should be 29.17% expended yet it is at 25.61%, an indication that the county
is on target and managing expenditures appropriately.

TREASURER’S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. She announced that a one-stop shop rabies clinic will be held at the

Clare County Animal Shelter on February 21, 2026 from 9:00 a.m. until 1:00 p.m.
Vaccinations and dog licenses will be available for purchase.

Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger also reported that her office, in partnership with the
Register of Deeds Office, can now electronically certify that property taxes have been
paid prior to a deed being recorded. Lastly, four different types of notices were recently
sent out in an effort to help residents in danger of foreclosure.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Chairman George Gilmore has attended his meetings. He reported that both of his
townships are in the midst of negotiations with the Clare County Road Commission.

VICE CHAIRMAN’S REPORT
Vice Chairman Rick Fancon had no report.

COMMISSIONER’S REPORT
Commissioner Housler has attended her meetings. She announced that Redding
Township is seeking a Supervisor and the board meetings have changed from the third
Wednesday to the third Monday of every month.
Commissioner Ambrozaitis reported that the Airport lights are almost complete.
Administrator Phelps was able to get the Michigan Department of Transportation
(MDOT) to claim a defective radio controller as part of the current grant. He also
shared that there is a tentative Easter Egg Drop scheduled for April 11, 2026 at the
Clare County Airport.
Commissioner Hoefling has attended his meetings and gave a report on each of the
townships and committees he represents. Franklin Township had a short meeting.
Hamilton Township continues to work on the park which is scheduled to be completed
in May. Lastly, he reported that Winterfield Township is seeking names for an
aSSeSsor.
Commissioner Haskell had no report.
Commissioner Gross had no report.
Commissioner Majewski had no report.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Dewayne Rogers, Manager of the Clare County Road Commission, announced that on
February 13, 2026 from 10:00 a.m. until 12:00 p.m. the Road Commission
Educational Workshop will take place at the Career and Technical Education (CTE)
Building.
A Clare County veteran thanked the Board of Commissioners for increasing the burial
funds for Veterans. He also expressed concern regarding the one-sided printing for the
Lake level documents that were distributed at a recent meeting.
