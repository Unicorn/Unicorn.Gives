ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps provided the Commissioners with updates from the
Michigan Association of Counties regarding county funding and transportation issues.
As the county approaches the fiscal year’s final quarter, the remaining annual
expenditure budget should be around 29.17% and it is currently at 37.43%. She
recently attended the Michigan Indigent Defense Commission (MIDC) meeting and
formally requested a contract amendment and additional funding to address the MIDC
program overage. The MIDC did approve an additional over $600,000.00 in funding for
the current fiscal year.

Administrator Phelps reported that Fiscal Year 2027 budget preparation is underway.
She has met with most department heads to review operational needs, staffing
considerations, capital requests and budget priorities. The county’s health insurance
renewal has returmed with a significant proposed increase; therefore, Administrator
Phelps is evaluating alternative options. Additionally, Materials Management Planning
continues to require a significant amount of staff time. The Clare County workgroup is
collecting and compiling data, developing preliminary goals, benchmarks and
implementation strategies. Administrator Phelps announced that she is scheduled to
meet with Gladwin County officials next week to discuss the shared court budgets and
ongoing cost sharing arrangements.

COMMISSIONER’S REPORT

Commissioner Hoefling has attended his meetings and gave a report on each of the
townships and committees he represents.

Commissioner Ambrozaitis attended the Mill Pond opening in Farwell and Midterm
Commissioners training at Kirkland College, stating both events were well done.

Commissioner Housler asked for an update regarding counts for spongy moths but
had no report.

Commissioner Kleinhardt commented on recent road worker deaths encouraging
drivers to slow down but had no report.

Commissioner Haskell reported that the Central Michigan District Health
Department’s Director Steve Hall resigned and they are looking for his replacement.

Commissioner Gross stated that he will be attending the Mid State Substance Use
Policy Board meeting in Lansing but had no report.

Commissioner Majewski reported that Lincoln Township is hosting a kick off for
Bertha Lakes on June 19, 2026 at 11 a.m. Shingle Lake Park’s construction is
underway and is scheduled to be complete end of September. Additionally,
Commissioner Majewski announced his retirement as Clare County District 2
Commissioner effective July 3, 2026.

PUBLIC COMMENT
A Clare County resident apologized to the Board for his actions at the previous Board
of Commissioners’ meeting. He expressed frustration that other speakers are
permitted to exceed the three-minute public comment limit, while he is consistently
stopped when he reaches the allotted three minutes. He also made a recommendation
that the Board of Commissioners resume the Committee of the Whole meetings.

Sheriff John Wilson, Administrator Lori Phelps and Commissioner Marlene Housler
expressed gratitude towards Commissioner Majewski for his service to Clare County.
