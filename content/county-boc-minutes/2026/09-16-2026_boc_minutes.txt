GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Several Clare County residents expressed concern regarding their addresses on West
Long Lake Drive being changed from Drive to Road. The address change is causing
significant issues affecting mail delivery, voter registration, emergency response and
beyond. Administrator Phelps recently attended a meeting with several other
organizations, and they are in the process of attempting to resolve the problem. Clare
County Road Commission Manager Dewayne Rogers stated that there is a committee
comprised of the Clare County Administration office, Equalization, 911 Dispatch and
the Building Inspector to work on address corrections. He asked for the concerned
residents to meet with him after the meeting for further discussion.

PUBLIC HEARINGS/SPECIAL PRESENTATIONS

1. Eric Karbowski Michigan State University (MSU) Extension
District 6 Director Eric Karbowski expressed gratitude for Clare County’s
continued support of MSU Extension. He gave a brief overview of the 2025

Master Gardner Program. He also introduced Renee Sanders, the 4-H
Program Coordinator who gave a report on recent 4-H events in Clare
County.

TREASURER’S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory finance reports and
stated that everything is reconciled up to this point. The local units have been busy
with the recent summer taxes deadline and her office has been busy with tax
adjustments from the July Board of Review. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger stated that
the schoolhouse project is completed. There will be an open house with the State Land
Bank and Lincoln Township Clerk Carol Majewski, invitations coming soon.
CLARE COUNTY ROAD COMMISSION
Manager Dewayne Rogers gave a report on the roadwork completed within the past
month throughout Clare County. He stated that they recently purchased chip seal
equipment so the Road Commissioner will be able to complete their own work. Lastly,
he reported that next Monday they are officially moving to the new building. A grand
opening is scheduled for September 30, 2026 from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. and lunch
will be provided.
