the final Health Insurance recommendation with the Board of

3. Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Budget Review
Undersheriff Ed Williams discussed a proposal in the Clare County Sheriff's
Department budget with respect to making changes to the rank and
command structure within the Clare County Jail.

Administrator Lori Phelps stated that the budget is balanced and went on to
review a few components. She stated that she would like to add the passing
of the FY 2027 Budget to the agenda for the September 16, 2026 Board of
Commissioners meeting.

Commissioner Gilmore asked for an itemized report to show the requested
increase for the Clare County Airport. Commissioner Ambrozaitis responded
as MCL 123.66A requires he will be submitting the report in October;
however, he will gladly give Commissioner Gilmore a personal copy
beforehand.
