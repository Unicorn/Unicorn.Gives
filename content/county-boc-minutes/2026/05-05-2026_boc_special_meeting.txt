APPROVAL OF AGENDA

It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner Gross to approve
the agenda, Motion carried.

GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Administrator Lori Phelps detailed the process of selecting BS&A as the cloud software
and services vendor. She also answered a few questions from the Commissioners
regarding the BS&A proposal.

FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

1. Software and Services Purchase
It was moved by Commissioner Fancon, seconded by Commissioner Housler to
approve the purchase of BS&A Cloud Software and Services in the amount of
$256,340.00 and have the Board Chair sign, roll call vote revealed:

Yeas: (7) being Commissioners Majewski, Housler, Haskell, Gross, Gilmore,
Fancon and Ambrozaitis

Nays: (0)

Absent: (2) Commissioners Hoefling and Kleinhardt

Motion carried.
