ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT-

Administrator Lori Phelps reported that State budgets and County funding proposals
are moving forward with suggestions of no increase for County revenue sharing. She
also shared updates regarding a few house bills in the works.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk & Register of Deeds Lori Mott informed the Board of Commissioners that she is
working with a couple of State and Nationwide work groups to combat ransomware,
malware and eliminate the risk of property fraud. As far as elections, Clerk & Register
of Deeds Mott reported that there will not be a May 2026 election. The Bureau of
Elections has not made a commitment to fund new election equipment and there is
not a requirement yet to purchase new equipment. Clerk & Register of Deeds Mott
plans to include replacement of election equipment in the 2027 budget in case the
State requires new equipment and does not provide funding.

Clerk & Register of Deeds Mott further reported that legislation is in the works to
change the primary election from August to May. If the change is approved it will not
go into effect until 2027 and the result will be having elections in February, May and
November. She listed the partial and full term positions throughout Clare County that
will be on the upcoming August Election ballot.

Clerk & Register of Deeds Mott shared several issues with the United States Postal
Service in regard to late mail and a recent change with how incoming mail is
postmarked. Unfortunately, the change will effect military and overseas voters because
we rely on postmarked dates to process ballots that are received after the election.
Lastly, she explained that her office will now be comparing incoming jury
questionnaires to their Qualified Voter File to ensure that any resident that states they
are not qualified to sit on a jury because they are not a United States citizen are not
registered to vote.

After a question from a Commissioner regarding property fraud, Clerk & Register of
Deeds Mott explained that there is a nationwide problem with property fraud with the
most common form of property fraud crimes being committed among family members.
The Register of Deeds office offers a free Property Fraud Alert service. Clare County
residents can visit the Register of Deeds website, call or stop into the office to sign up
for the free service. She recommended signing up individual names, business names
and trusts.
