GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Dewayne Rogers, Clare County Road Commissioner Manager, stated that he brought
in the Road Commission’s Spring Newsletter for everyone to read. He expressed his
gratitude for Jerry Becker, Director of the Clare County Emergency Services Homeland
Security Division, for his help during the recent flooding. Additionally, Dewayne
Rogers reported that the Road Commission’s construction season is underway with
paving, culvert replacement and gravel road projects.

A Hayes Township resident shared concern that the video recordings of previous
meetings are difficult to watch. He mentioned that people’s faces are cut off, the audio
is hard to hear and the camera does not follow faces.

A member of the public asked in general what criteria determines whether an issue
goes to a people vote versus a planning commission. The response was to fill out a
form and a written response will be given.

A Hayes Township resident sited a few instances in previous meetings where it
appears the Commissioners have a lack of time to prepare so they are fully informed
on issues they will vote on. He suggested the Board of Commissioners return to having
the Committee of the Whole meetings or have two regular meetings a month in order
to better prepare.

Several Clare County residents expressed their opposition to the proposed recycling

transfer station in Hayes Township. They requested that the Board of Commissioners
withdraw their support.

ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps provided the Commissioners with legislative and financial
updates from the State. She reported on several House bills regarding housing
development, planning infrastructure and public safety decisions. The initial on-
boarding meeting with BS&A took place last week; the projection date to go live is
December 7, 2026. Administrator Phelps stated that the State has rolled out the
public safety revenue sharing program for cities, villages and townships.

She reported that work continues on the County’s Materials Management Plan. She
recently sent survey requests to the Clare County Cleaver, Clare County elected
officials and posted it on the Clare County website. It is important that Clare County
residents participate and complete the survey before the end of the month. Lastly,
Administrator Phelps stated that she completed the Deficit Elimination Plan for the
Michigan Indigent Defense Fund but recently discovered that she needs to submit it
with a resolution, which is why there is an addition to the agenda today.

SHERIFF’S REPORT

Sheriff John Wilson presented the Board of Commissioners with a year-to-date report.
His report included information from the City of Clare Police Department, the Clare
County Animal Shelter, Fire and Rescue and other departments. Sheriff Wilson
mentioned that the Clare County Dispatch is currently understaffed, however the road
patrol is fully staffed. He went into detail with other staffing arrangements and
equipment upgrades. Lastly, Sheriff Wilson reported that construction for the sally
port is scheduled to begin in July.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Commissioner on their desks.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Chairman George Gilmore has attended his meetings. He stated that Grant Township
was pleased to learn that Clare County decided to go with BS&A Cloud.

VICE CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Vice Chairman Rick Fancon asked if there is county-wide support for Clare County
homeless residents. Administrator Phelps gave the phone number for Mid-Michigan
Community Action.

COMMISSIONER’S REPORT

Commissioner Housler has attended her meetings. She announced that Garfield
Township is hosting a car wash on Saturday, May 23, 2026.

Commissioner Hoefling gave a reminder that the grand opening for Hamilton
Township’s park will be June 6, 2026. The ribbon cutting ceremony begins at 11:30
a.m. and food will be served from 12:00 p.m. until 4:00 p.m. He also mentioned that
several of his townships continue to voice their opposition to data centers.

Commissioner Majewski has attended his meetings. He announced that the Lincoln
Township Fire Department is hosting a pancake breakfast fundraiser on Saturday,
May 23, 2026 from 9:00 a.m. until 12:00 p.m.

Commissioner Gross had nothing to report.
Commissioner Haskell had nothing to report.

Commissioner Kleinhardt asked if an explanation could be provided on how the
Materials Management Planning Committee needs to come up with a plan for recycling
due to the possibility of Lansing eliminating the Michigan Bottle Bill. Administrator
Phelps responded that she can put together a summary of the information currently
gathered and present it at the next meeting. Commissioner Kleinhardt emphasized
that the recycling center is not a done deal.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Clare County residents reiterated their opinions against the proposed recycling
transfer station.

A member of the public expressed an opinion that the Board of Commissioners’
meetings need to be moved to the afternoon. He also stated that Commissioner Fancon
has made statements at previous meetings that if the majority of the people did not
want it, he would have it stopped. He encouraged people to complete the survey on the
Clare County website as Administrator Phelps asked.

A Hayes Township resident asked if the phone number to Mid-Michigan Community
Action could be distributed to the townships to put on their websites. He added that
he believes that the Board of Commissioners will do what is right for the community.

A member of the public expressed concern regarding the new parks in the area. She
questioned whether or not the maintenance of the parks will be funded through
taxpayers’ funds, even though the parks were built with grant funds.

A Clare County resident clarified the rules the County Board of Commissioners should
follow in the event a change needs to be made to the Board of County Road
Commissioners. He also explained the difference between Committee of the Whole
meetings and Regular meetings. Lastly, he noted that during the March 18, 2026
meeting members of the public were given more than the allotted three minutes to
speak and he hopes that the Board continues to keep to the three minutes in the
future.

Commissioner Hoefling stated that he agrees with the return of the Committee of the
Whole meetings.
