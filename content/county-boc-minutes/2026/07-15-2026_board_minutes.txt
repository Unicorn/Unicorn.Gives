ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps took some time to explain transparency, public service and
the importance of the public having all facts before drawing conclusions. She clearly
stated that she supports the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and believes it is an
important tool that promotes open government and accountability. Administrator
Phelps will begin providing the Board of Commissioners a summary of the FOIA
requests received for better understanding of the volume of requests, amount of staff
time involved in gathering records, reviewing documents, responding and the cost to
taxpayers.

Administrator Phelps expressed concern that at times only part of the information
received from a FOIA request is shared publicly without the context of the complete
story. Information shared in this manner may suggest wrongdoing or conflicts of
interest. She stated that public accountability is essential and should be based on
complete information. She remains committed to being transparent and complying to
every FOIA request.
