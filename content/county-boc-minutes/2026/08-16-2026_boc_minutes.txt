APPROVAL OF AGENDA

It was moved by Commissioner Ambrozaitis, seconded by Commissioner Fancon to
approve the agenda, motion carried.

APPROVAL OF MINUTES

It was moved by Commissioner Haskell, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling to approve
the Board of Commissioners Minutes of July 15, 2026, the Board of Commissioners
Special Meeting Minutes of July 7, 2026 and August 5, 2026, motion carried.

GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

A member of the public requested information regarding the 2024 Prosecutor vs. Clare
County lawsuit and made suggestions regarding the airport budget. Another member
of the public commented regarding the airport.

Clare County Road Commission Manager Dewayne Rogers reported that he will give
the Board of Commissioners a monthly update to improve communication between the
Board and the Road Commission. He proceeded to introduce Brent Lynch, the sign
shop foreman. Brent Lynch is the direct contact at the Road Commission for questions
or concerns regarding signs, speed limit issues, speed studies, traffic concerns, etc.
Lastly, Dewayne Rogers shared a detailed update regarding the work completed so far
this year in the county and the purchase of the Warner building and surrounding
twenty eight acres.

COMMUNICATIONS LIST
1. Medical Examiner Monthly Report
ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT
Administrator Lori Phelps reported that the Heartland Materials Management Group
completed the work on the Materials Management Plan. The completed plan will be

brought before the Board during the September meeting. She encouraged the
Commissioners to reach out to her with any questions on the proposed budget.

TREASURER’S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory finance reports to the
Commissioners. She pointed out that the General Fund balance will begin to increase
since the fourth quarter is when the largest amount of revenue comes in. All twenty
six of the available properties sold during the County’s recent public land sale. Lastly,
Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger reported that the Land Bank demolition projects are
complete and the rehabilitation project in Lincoln Township is almost complete.
