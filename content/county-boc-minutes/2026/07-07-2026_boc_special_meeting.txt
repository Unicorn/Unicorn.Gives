APPOINTMENT VOTE OR DEFER UNTIL REGULAR BOARD MEETING

 

Commissioner Gilmore suggested taking an initial vote via ballot to determine if a clear
winner stood out prior to discussing all three applicants. Applicant Bob Keasey won the
majority vote of 5/3 and is thereby appointed Clare County District 2 Commissioner.
Commissioner Hoefling recommended a second vote via ballot to show a unanimous
decision among the Board of Commissioners. That second ballot vote was for Bob
Keasey by unanimous vote 8/0.

County Commissioner names are on each voted ballot to identify how each
Commissioner voted so the public can poll the votes if requested.
