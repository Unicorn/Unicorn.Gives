JUSTICE

1. Undersheriff Williams — Sally Port Discussion
Clare County Undersheriff Ed Williams addressed the Board of
Commissioners regarding the Clare County Sheriff's Department desire for a
newly constructed sally port. A sally port is a controlled area where inmates
are securely transferred between vehicles and the Sheriffs Department
building. The current area lacks the appropriate amount of space needed to
accommodate multiple vehicles, including an ambulance. The proposed sally
port would allow capacity for up to three vehicles and also provide the
Sheriffs Department an area needed for the body scanner approved for
purchase in 2024. Funding for the project will initially be paid from the
Sheriffs Department General fund and will be reimbursed through the
Commissary fund.

After some discussion, it was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by
Commissioner Housler to approve the project for the sally port and scanner,
roll call vote revealed:

Yeas: (8) being Commissioners Ambrozaitis, Gilmore, Gross, Haskell, Hoefling,
Housler, Kleinhardt and Majewski

Nays: (0)

Absent: (1) being Commissioner Fancon

Motion carried.

ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps reported that Governor Whitmer presented the proposed
2027 budget on February 13, 2026. The County revenue sharing numbers remain the.
same. Several grants are on the horizon including one to assist with cybersecurity
funding. She shared that the request to Michigan Indigent Defense Commission
(MIDC) to use grant funds for cost allocation (to cover the cost of administering the
grant) was denied with no reason for the denial. Lastly, Administrator Phelps reported
that the budget should be 41.67% expensed; however, the county is currently 32.41%
expensed.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Commissioners showing the current cash, investments and fund balances. Her office
is prepared for the Rabies Clinic which will be held this Saturday, February 21, 2026.
She reminded the Commissioners that the tax deadlines at the local and county levels
are coming soon.

NEW BUSINESS
Country’s Motto/Ten Commandments Discussion
Commissioner Gilmore opened discussion on displaying the National Motto and Ten
Commandments in the courthouse. He explained that in 2019 the Supreme Court
ruled that the National Motto and Ten Commandments are allowed in all public
buildings. Commissioner Ambrozaitis replied that he would like to see the court
ruling. Commissioner Majewski added that there are different versions of the Ten
Commandments and selecting one version over another may upset people.

It was moved by Commissioner Gilmore to go ahead and do this, there was no second
to the motion, motion failed.
