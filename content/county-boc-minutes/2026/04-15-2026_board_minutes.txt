ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps stated that there are minimal legislative updates to report
because of Spring Break. The two year price lock with the current health insurance
provider is set to expire soon so Administrator Phelps is going through the bidding
process. She reported that the County Audit is complete and she is in progress with
several grants.

Additionally, Administrator Phelps announced that a total of three bids for the
comprehensive software solution project have been submitted and further review is
required for two companies that submitted bids late in the process. One of the
companies is significantly less than the current company used, BS&A Cloud Software.
The Recovery Court programming has expanded because of recent Opioid Settlement
funding. Lastly, Administrator Phelps reported the County budget is historically
54.17% expensed at this time of the year; it is currently 46% expensed indicating that
the County remains in a strong financial position.

UNFINISHED BUSINESS

NEW BUSINESS

. Road Commission Discussion

The Board of Commissioners discussed whether the Board of County Road
Commissioners should have five board members or be reduced back to three
board members. The intent for increasing to five board members was to have
better communication between the Road Commission and the townships.

Due to a lack of township meeting attendance from some of the Road
Commission board members, communication has not improved. It was
decided to give the Road Commission board an opportunity to improve their
attendance at township meetings before the Clare County Board of
Commissioners make a decision to reduce back to three board members.

. Windows Discussion

Administrator Phelps shared pictures of the bug infested and rotted
windows on the Clare County Courthouse building. She informed the Board
of Commissioners that she currently has one bid to replace some windows
and will work on receiving one more bid before presenting it to the Board.

. GFL Expansion Discussion and Support Request

Administrator Phelps read a letter of support for a proposed transfer station
with an integrated recycling component in Hayes Township. After
Commissioner Fancon answered questions from the Board of
Commissioners and members of the public in attendance, the Board signed
the letter of support.

. City of Harrison Zoning Concerns Discussion
