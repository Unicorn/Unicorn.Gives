ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps provided the Board of Commissioners with updates from the
Governor's office suggesting 5% increase for revenue sharing, which would total
$80,000.00. There is a state Medicaid program being introduced which would pay for
medical expenses for inmates. In addition House Bill 1115 is being introduced, which
would allow federal Medicaid to cover expenses of inmates while in jail. Middle
Michigan Development has a new representative, Allison Heinrick and Administrator
Phelps has been named Vice-Chair of the East Michigan Council of Governments.

Administrator Phelps informed the Board of Commissioners that the State of Michigan

EGLE is now requiring counties to put a composting plan together. Administrator
Phelps and Commissioner Fancon are working together on that plan.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott gave an update regarding Clare County's first
ever early voting with 58 voters utilizing the service as of Tuesday evening. With the
help of members of her team/staff (Stacy, Christina, Pam and Brittany), six Local
Clerks (Hatton, Frost, Hayes, Harrison, Clare and Hamilton), four Deputy Clerks
(Frost, Garfield. Sheridan and Clare) and four security guards (Brent, Jamie, Paul and
Brian) early voting has been successful.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. Her office is in the middle of tax season and reminded the public that
the deadline on a judicial foreclosure, to ask for extension from judge, is February 22,
2022 and urged anyone in danger of losing their property to contact her office because
she can still work with them until the March 31, 2024 deadline.

Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger also reminded everyone that the deadline to purchase a
dog license without a late fee is February 29, 2024.

COMMISSIONER'S REPORT - continued

Commissioner Ambrozaitis also has had a lot of feedback on the ambulance service
issue, confirmed the next meeting date for the airport committee and reported on the
expansion of the road commission.

Commissioner Gilmore also questioned when the next meeting on the ambulance
service will be held.

Commissioner Fancon met with Isabella County to work together on Part 115, which
is the path to zero landfills. He reported that as part of that bill there will be hidden
taxes and fees that will be passed onto consumer. The State of Michigan has lowest

cost for out of state landfills to dump in Michigan, which aaa many other states and
Canada to bring their garbage to Michigan.

STATE OF THE COUNTY BUDGET
Administrator Phelps reported that as of the end of February the budget would
normally be expected to be 42% expended and it is currently at 37.19% expended.

She will continue to monitor the budget and report any progress or issues.

Administrator Phelps is working on the 2025 Budget and will be bringing PA 152 costs
and eight union negotiations to the Board of Commissioners.

PUBLIC COMMENT
One member of the public asked if the 2% of revenue sharing that must be used on
public safety could be used for increased ambulance services. Administrator Phelps
confirmed that could happen, but that would only be $1,580.00.
