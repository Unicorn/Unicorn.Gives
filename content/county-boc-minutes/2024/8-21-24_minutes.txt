4. Health Insurance

a.

It was moved by Commissioner Fancon, seconded by Commissioner
Hoefling to opt out of Public Act 152, Chairman Haskell asked for
discussion, Commissioner Gilmore thought that combining jobs
through attrition in an effort to fully insure the rest of the employees
is not a good idea and indicated that people doing two jobs and being
paid for one job will not be happy for any length of time.
Commissioner Gilmore suggested having all of the employees pay a
little more of the costs and keep the existing positions. Commissioner
Kleinhardt expressed concern regarding the increasing cost of
providing health insurance. Administrator Phelps explained again, as
she has in previous meetings, that if the Commissioners opted out of
Public Act 152 and voted to adopt the Priority Health Insurance Plan
the costs to the employees would remain the same as the current
year, the coverage for the employees would remain the same as the
current year and the County would see a cost savings in the amount
of $68,000.00, which, as discussed previously, that savings would be
passed onto the employees. Commissioner Ambrozaitis asked
Administrator Phelps to confirm that if this motion to opt out of
Public Act 152 does not pass the employees would see a significant
increase in their health insurance costs. Administrator Phelps
confirmed that the employees would see a significant increase in their
cost for health insurance and the County would see a significant
savings. Administrator Phelps also confirmed that the employees
recognize and understand that the health insurance is a good benefit
and appreciate it. Chairman Haskell confirmed that all the unions,
during negotiations, took the benefit package plan into consideration
when they accepted a lower wage. All union negotiations are
complete and ready for acceptance pending this motion passing and if
this motion does not pass the negotiations will fall apart.

Administrator Phelps formally recommended the Board of
Commissioners opt out of PA 152 and pass the motion to accept the
Priority Health Insurance Plan.

Chairman Haskell recommended forming a committee to research and
discuss changes to the health insurance and address it in the next
budget year instead of hitting the employees in the pocketbook today
by increasing their health insurance costs in addition to the high
inflation they are already combating. Roll call vote revealed:

Yeas: (3) being Commissioners Hoefling, Fancon and Haskell

Nays: (3) being Commissioners Housler, Gilmore and Kleinhardt
Abstained (2) being Commissioners Majewski and Ambrozaitis both
gave the reason of direct financial benefit through spouses that work
for Clare County

Absent: (1) being Commissioner Pitchford

Motion failed

6. Clerk Discussion — Ballot Programming
Clerk/ Register of Deeds/ Abstractor Lori Mott presented to the Board of
Commissioners a request for compensation for programming the ballot for
elections. Clerk Mott described the additional time commitment that is put
into programming and the additional election duties that have been placed
on the County Clerk’s office over the past several years. Clerk Mott
presented two options:

a. A salary increase of $13,000.00 for the County Clerk/ Register of
Deeds/ Abstractor and $7,000.00 for the Chief Deputy County
Clerk/ Register of Deeds / Abstractor.

b. A stipend for programming of $9,000 for the County Clerk/Register of
Deeds/ Abstractor and $5,000.00 for the Chief Deputy County
Clerk/Register of Deeds/Abstractor. A stipend would only be paid
when there is an election and ballot programming is necessary.

Clerk Mott explained the difficulty she is having in keeping the election
position filled in her office explaining the extreme stress surrounding
elections. Clerk Mott suggested not filling the election position and
transferring those funds to pay for an increase for the County

Clerk/ Register of Deeds/ Abstractor, County Prosecuting Attorney and
County Treasurer. Clerk Mott went on to confirm that the three women,
through Prosecutor Ambrozaitis, asked for an increase in wages a few years
prior when the County Sheriff and Undersheriff both received increases, but
that request was never granted.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott informed the Commissioners that there have
been two recounts filed for the County Commissioner District 7 race and Hayes
Township Trustee race. Clerk and Register of Deeds Mott also gave election law
changes and updates, privacy shielding law updates, jury selection law updates and
notary law updates that are all currently taking place. Clerk and Register of Deeds
Mott explained the proactive actions being taken in the Register of Deeds office in
preparation of the privacy shielding law changes.

Clerk and Register of Deeds Mott expressed her great sadness and disappointment in
the Board of Commissioners for not opting out of PA 152 which results in a huge
increase in costs to the employees. She confirmed how hard the employees of Clare
County work and the health insurance was one benefit that kept employees working
here. Clerk and Register of Deeds Mott encouraged the County Commissioners to talk
with the empleyees and hear how their decision on health insurance will greatly affect
the lives of the employees.
