ADMINISTRATOR'S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps reported that the wage committee has met one time and she
has finished the wage study for elected officials and chief deputies, they will meet one
more time before the December Board meeting. The Michigan Department of
Corrections (MDOC) Jail inspection is complete and 100% compliant with the rules
and regulations set forth by the State of Michigan. The House Judiciary Committee
heard testimony regarding eliminating cash bail and moving to release on own
recognizance and handed out information regarding the testimony.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott gave an update regarding the November
Election. Early voting ran very smoothly with a total of 2,618 voters. Voters
commented that they like early voting because of the convenience. The Board of
Canvassers are on their fourth day of Canvassing the Election.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners.
