GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Members of the public thanked all members of the Board that have always supported
the Airport and asked that they continue to support the airport.

Other members of the public spoke regarding their knowledge of elections and the
extra hours that the County Clerk and her staff work in which they are not
compensated. They are also concerned about losing the integrity of the election if the
programming of the election is sent out of county to a vendor instead of being done by
the County Clerk. There was also concern regarding paying more than double toa
large out of county company for a service that is already being done in house by a
local elected official.

3. Budget Fiscal Year 2025

It was moved by Commissioner Gilmore, seconded by Commissioner
Hoefling to adopt Resolution #24-17, approving and adopting the County
General Appropriation Act for Fiscal Year 2025, Chairman Haskell asked for
discussion, Commissioner Fancon stated none of the constituents in his
district support funding the County Airport because only one township and
city benefit. Commissioner Fancon went on to confirm that if the
commissioners are not good stewards of the taxpayers money then they
cannot expect the department heads and elected officials to be good
stewards of tax payer money. Guided by Commissioner Gilmore,
Commissioner Fancon moved, seconded by Commissioner Gilmore to amend
the motion to suspend financing for the airport. Commissioner Ambrozaitis
recognized Commissioner Fancon’s continued absence from the Airport
Committee meetings that he was appointed to and his failure to attend,
while continuing to accept pay. Commissioner Hoefling questioned why
there was movement to amend an already balanced budget. Rol! call vote to
amend the motion revealed:

Yeas: (5} being Commissioners Housler, Majewski, Gilmore, Kleinhardt and Fancon
Nays: (3) Hoefling, Ambrozaitis and Haskell

Absent: (1) being Samantha Pitchford

Motion carried
