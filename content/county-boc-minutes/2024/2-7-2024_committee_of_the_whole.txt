PUBLIC COMMENT

Members of the public made suggestions on how medical services should be provided,
how to fund emergency medical services and provided stories of personal incidents.
Emergency Medical Personnel commented on what actually happens in an emergency
situation and made suggestions of what services should be in place.

All 5 Fire Chiefs in Clare County were present and some of them provided input on the
subject. The Fire Departments are called to help Mobile Medical Response (MMR) on
many occasions.

Questions were asked including if a millage would add additional ambulances or fund
the ones that are already stationed in Clare County.

Grant Township Supervisor Dan Dysinger and Greenwood Township Supervisor Jess
McClaughry spoke against a county-wide millage for ambulance service.

JUSTICE

1. Public Hearing Regarding Emergency Medical Services
County Administrator Lori Phelps explained the purpose of the meeting and
set up guidelines for the meeting.

It was explained that non-emergency use of ambulance services in Clare
County is part of the problem. Some people call the ambulance to take
them to the hospital because they don’t have a vehicle or family members to
take them to the doctor, so they call an ambulance and go to the hospital.

Commissioner Haskell confirmed that Clare County does not currently
contribute any funds toward ambulance services.

MMR Representative Eric Snidersich VP of Operations confirmed they spent
$4 million last year servicing Clare County and confirmed that the transfers
they provide help pay the bills. Without transfers they would have been
short $1.7 million last year. He also explained that in the event there were 6
calls at one time, MMR would pull resources from other areas to
accommodate. The cost to MMR is $400.00 per hour to have an ambulance
sitting before any resources are used.

JUSTICE - continued

1. Public Hearing Regarding Emergency Medical Services - continued

MMR is trying to increase staff by paying people to go through EMT school
and guarantee a job when they get complete the program. They also
confirmed that only 5% of 911 calls are life threatening. Medical Board
Authority Chairman Glenn King noted that 95% of response times are15
minutes or under. If an ambulance was stationed in every township the
response time would improve, but that is not financially sustainable.

New to the EMS Community is a Paramedicine Model where paramedics
partner with doctors to take care of issue without transporting to a hospital.
This option is being looked at and not currently available.

It was agreed to form a committee and have a meeting with county
commissioner board members, MMR, Medical Board Authority and
Township Supervisors and talk about options.

Administrator Phelps will send a letter and also ask for participation to the
fire chiefs and 911 dispatchers. Commissioners Hoefling, Housler and
Majewski will be on the Committee.

PHYSICAL RESOURCES

1. Discussion Middle Michigan Development Corporation
In the past Clare County contributed $22,000.00 per year to Middle
Michigan Development and then reduced their contribution to $12,500.00
per year. Middie Michigan Development Corporation is requesting Clare
County reinstate funding to $22,000.00 per year. Administrator Phelps
handed out their annual report.

It was moved by Commissioner Majewski. seconded by Commissioner Heefling to
adjourn, motion carried.
