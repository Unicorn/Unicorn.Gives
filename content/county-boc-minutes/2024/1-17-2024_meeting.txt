PUBLIC HEARINGS /SPECIAL PRESENTATIONS

1. Honorable Joshua M. Farrell
Judge Farrell informed the Board of Commissioners that in November of
2023 the Michigan Supreme Court appointed him for a two-year term as the
Chief Judge over the 80% District Court and the 55th Circuit Court. As a part
of that appointment he has had meetings with department heads to see
what was working and what was not working. He also began working on a
court restructure plan where he looked at number of things that could be
improved. Judge Farrell has seen other county departments restructuring
their offices and have taken on more responsibilities with fewer resources
and less staff. He has restructured some employees in courts and was able
to consolidate some positions and eliminate some positions in an effort to
accomplish the same. Mr. Worpell will now be Court Administrator for both
Clare and Gladwin Counties and Magistrate for both counties. Judge Farrell
finished up by confirming he wanted to personally make this announcement
while kicking off the New Year and answer any questions.
