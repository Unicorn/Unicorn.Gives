GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

 

Mr. Raymond Elliott was present and brought his concern regarding the health issues
he believes are being caused by the landfill. Mr. Elliott confirmed that he has
contacted the Health Department and the EPA (Environmental Protection Agency) with
no resolution. Commissioner Majewski confirmed that the Board of Commissioners
has no authority to do anything about the alleged contamination of the air and ground
by the landfill other than to keep pushing other agencies to do something and
confirmed that the Board of Commissioners have been pushing the agencies to act.

ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

 

Administrator Lori Phelps provided the Commissioners with updates on House Bills
being considered and regarding the Governor's budget and revenue sharing. There
has been a great deal of thought and effort into alternate revenue sources including
consolidating jail facilities with Osceola County with a possible start date of J uly 1,
2024,

Administrator Phelps is working with Department Heads regarding restructuring of
departments and has asked a few department heads not to replace employees’ vacant
positions.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott gave an update regarding preparing the August
6, 2024 ballot. There will be 16 proposals on the August ballot. Clerk and Register of
Deeds Mott confirmed that the cost for early voting in Clare County was $48,126.77.

Clerk and Register of Deeds Mott spoke regarding Secretary of State Benson's Intent to
Negotiate in regard to a single source supplier for Michigan's logic and accuracy
testing for all election equipment. Clerk and Register of Deeds Mott expressed her
concern and dissatisfaction with the lack of communication with the Michigan
Association of County Clerks in regard to this decision.

TREASURER’'S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners and confirmed that 2023 ended with a $4.9 million fund balance

unrestricted, which is a 33% fund balance.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

 

Chairman Jeff Haskell confirmed that he feels combining Osceola County jail with the
Clare County jail would be a good idea and will give regular updates as the
meetings/talks continue.
