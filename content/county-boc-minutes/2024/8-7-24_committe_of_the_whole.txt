HUMAN AND SOCIAL SERVICE

1. Housing Needs Assessment
Administrator Phelps confirmed that there is a housing shortage in Clare
County and that Grants are difficult to get when a county doesn’t have a
Housing Needs Assessment. Administrator helps would like to partner with
other counties and have a joint assessment completed. A Housing Needs
Assessment cost is generally $58,000.00 and Administrator Phelps would
like to contribute $5,000.00 on behalf of Clare County out of Program
Income dollars in Community Development. Chairman Haskell agreed to
have this issue placed on the August Agenda.

PHYSICAL RESOURCES

1. Materials Management Planning
Clare County is required to implement a Materials Management Plan within
the next three years that will take the place of the Solid Waste Plan.
Counties have been encouraged by EAGLE to partner with other counties to
develop this plan. Clare County has joined together with Gladwin, Osceola,
Mecosta, Isabella and Gratiot Counties and has been meeting twice a month
developing committees and bylaws. Each County is required to appoint a
Designated Planning Agent (DPA). Administrator Phelps has been acting as
the DPA, but the Commissioners will have to appoint her, a County
Commissioner and one other community member at the next Board of
Commissioner Meeting as DPA’s.

FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

1. Two Factor Authentication
The State of Michigan is requiring that all law enforcement information
networks are to have two factor authentications in place no later than
October 1, 2024 as an additional security measure. Administrator Phelps
noted that there is funding in the computer budget to fund the $22,000.00
costs. Chairman Haskell will place this item on the agenda for the next
meeting.

2. Health Insurance Discussion
Priority Health submitted a quote for health insurance since the last
presentation that offers a significant savings to the County ($68,000.00) and
employee contributions would stay the same as they are currently.

Administrator Phelps also discussed the option of offering an employee a
$700.00 per month payment if the employee does not take the health
insurance or a health saving account (HSA) instead of taking the health
insurance. That would come to approximately $165,000.00 savings.

Blue Care Network (BCN) would create an increase in cost to the County of
7.5% in addition to an increase to the employees.

Commissioner Gilmore mentioned that the nice thing about not opting out of
PA 152 would allow more flexibility in negotiating with the
employees/unions because the employees see their wages and don’t always
consider what a good insurance plan they have or what it saves them.
Administrator Phelps confirmed that PA 152 would be a cost savings of
$338,000.00 to the county and a significant increase to the employees.
Commissioner Majewski does not believe the cost burden should be placed
entirely on the employees.
