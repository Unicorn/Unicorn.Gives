It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner Gilmore to
approve the agenda, motion carried.

It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling to
approve the minutes of the Committee of the Whole Meeting on November 6, 2024 and
the Board of Commissioners Meeting of November 13, 2024, motion carried.

GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Citizens expressed concern regarding gas-like odors, black substances covering their
plants, apparent spills of black substance in the roadway in their neighborhood,
passed around photographs of each and asked for the Board of Commissioners for
help in getting other government agencies to respond to their continued complaints.

COMMUNICATIONS LIST
1. City of Harrison Master Plan update - No discussion
ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps provided the Commissioners with updates regarding items
being looked at by the legislators, changes that are needed to county policies, the
county audit, grants she is perusing, Michigan Municipal Risk Management Authority
(MMRMA) insurance renewal, the wage study she is working on (she will have a
summary for each employee), alternate funding for trial courts study currently ongoing
and the Standard & Poor’s (S&P) credit rating interview for the Road Commission
Bond request. Administrator Phelps informed the Board Members that they would be
voting on a resolution for the Road Commission Bond at the January 15, 2025
meeting.

. Wage Discussion
a. It was moved by Commissioner Kleinhardt, seconded by

Commissioner Hoefling to increase the salaries of both the Clare
County Clerk and Treasurer to $75,000.00 per year as recommended
by the Wage Committee, Administrator Phelps confirmed she looked
at every county in the state and compared the population, taxable
value and millage rates, which narrowed it down to 14 counties. Out
of those 14 counties she then compared the wages, benefit packages,
duties and office size to come up with the average wages. In addition
Commissioner Haskell added and Administrator Phelps confirmed
that the Clerk and Treasurer made the same wage in all the
comparable counties, roll call vote revealed:

Yeas: (7) being Commissioners Kleinhardt, Hoefling, Ambrozaitis,
Gilmore, Fancon, Kleinhardt, Majewski and Haskell

Nays: (0)

Absent: (2) being Commissioners Fancon and Pitchford

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott introduced a new employee Isha Walter. Clerk
and Register of Deeds Mott informed the Board of Commissioners that she has
received the reimbursement she requested from the State of Michigan for the
Presidential Primary in the amount of $59,000.00 and for the August Primary Election
in the amount of $9,000.00, which goes back into the General Fund. Clerk and
Register of Deeds Mott also informed the Board of Commissioners that at a recent
conference the Bureau of Elections confirmed there is no discussion regarding
reimbursing wages for early voting in the future and they do not expect any such
reimbursement in the future. She also updated them on legislation that would
increase election costs at the county and local level regarding curbside voting and
bilingual ballots.

VICE CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Vice Chairman Dale Majewski reported that he has been attending his meetings and

want to purchase the pass.

COMMISSIONERS’ REPORT
Commissioner Hoefling gave a report on each of the township meetings he attended.

Commissioner Ambrozaitis welcomed and introduced the new Hayes Township
Supervisor, Brenden Powell.

Commissioner Gilmore had no report.

Commissioner Kleinhardt reported on the vast number of house bills being proposed
in Lansing by the State Legislators.

Commissioner Fancon had no report.
STATE OF THE COUNTY BUDGET

Administrator Phelps reported that the unaudited fund balance is $5.8 million and
sits at 39% of the full county budget.

PUBLIC COMMENT

A community member expressed his appreciation for the new public address (PA)
system that is coming soon, gave comment on how nice the swearing in ceremony for
the elected officials was, asked for consideration regarding the upcoming selection of
committee members that all three Airport Committee members were pro-airport unlike
the current committee. He made comments regarding the requested stipend for the
County Clerk and thought there should be a study regarding the difference in cost for
the Clerk or Election Source to program the ballots and asked that the Commissioners
support the Road Commission Bond request.

A community member asked for help at the South end of Harrison regarding the toxic
substance he believes is being dispersed.
