PUBLIC HEARINGS /SPECIAL PRESENTATIONS

1, Pete Preston, Director of Equalization
Director Preston described the culmination of the equalization process and
how the assessment process between the Equalization Department and the
local Assessors. Director Preston is asking that the Board of Commissioners
approve the L-4024, which is entire set of data for each local unit added up
for the County’s final equalization that will be sent to the State of Michigan.

Director Preston reported that Clare County has had a sizable increase in
market value which affects the assessed value. Clare County’s assessed
value has increased by 17%. There was also a high inflation rate, which
drives up the taxable value.

TREASURER'S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger has settled with all the local units for
the 2023 tax year. Her office has also sent out just under 6,000 notices. It looks like
this year’s auction of property will be the lowest she has ever seen at under 30
properties.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT
Chairman Jeff Haskell reported that on April 3, 2024 the CTE program had a job fair
with 30-40 local businesses available to meet with the students. He also reported that
Clare County's unemployment rate is still at 6.7%.
