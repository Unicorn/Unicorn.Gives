GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Members of the public spoke regarding their support for the Road Commission’s
request to build a new building.

Members of the public spoke regarding their dissatisfaction with how the defunding of
the county-owned airport was handled, specifically by not including the Airport
Committee and with no discussion with the people and businesses that have signed
leases with Clare County that are directly affected by the defunding of the airport.

Multiple members of the public spoke that purchased property in Clare County
specifically because of the County Airport.

Members of the public spoke in support of defunding the county-owned airport and do
not feei that it will bring people to the County, referenced the fact that the County
does not support the City of Clare owned airport.

The owner of the Airport Restaurant spoke regarding how defunding the County
owned airport will affect her business, the people she employes the Clare County
residents that frequent her restaurant and the people from out of town that fly in and
drive into Clare County to spend their money here. She also questioned of who is
going to compensate her for the 10-years of lost business, sighting that she just signed
a 10-year contract with Clare County to lease the building that houses her restaurant.

One member of the public offered to research and talk on behalf of the Clare County to

inquire if an agreement can be made between City of Harrison, Hayes Township and
Clare County to jointly fund the County owned airport.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Clare County Road Commission Manager Dewayne Rogers offered to meet and have
discussions regarding the potential use of Road Commission equipment to help with
the airport and eliminate some of the Commissioner’s concerns regarding the County
owned airport.

There were additional comments regarding the differences between the County owned
airport and the airport owned by the City of Clare, the consequences of giving up the
airport that has been in existence since 1937 and never being able to get it back,
suggestions regarding ways to fund the airport and the possibility of making the
airport private and not a public airport.

Questions arose regarding the next committee meeting regarding ambulance services
for Clare County. No meeting is currently scheduled.

There were comments regarding dissatisfaction and fiscal irresponsibility with the
Commissioner’s 7-2 vote at the last meeting to pay a company out of Grand Rapids for
Clare County ballot programming instead of keeping it in house and in county and pay
the County Clerk a stipend to continue the ballot programming at a fraction of the
cost.
