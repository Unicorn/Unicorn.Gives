GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Kim Kennicott (former Airport Manager) spoke regarding funding the airport and
commended the efforts by Commissioner Ambrozaitis to improve the airport. Mr.
Kennicott asked the Commissioners to fund and maintain the airport to meet FAA
(Federal Aviation Administration) standards, as he indicated that the Board of
Commissioners has not done in years past. Mr. Kennicott asked the Board of
Commissioners to accept the free money being offered by a grant to be presented later
in the meeting.

Pat Adams spoke in support of the airport and pointing out that the life flight can land
there and know exactly where to go to land and meet the ambulance. Mr. Adams
asked how much one life is worth to the Commissioners. Mr. Adams confirmed that
communities with airports expand more than communities without an airport.

Carl Lounsbury owns one of the hangers at the airport and just signed 7-year lease
and feels he is under a constant threat by the Board of Commissioners to close the
airport. Hanger owners pay taxes on hangers and pay lease payments on hangers. Mr.
Lounsbury pointed out that if the airport wasn’t a viable asset to community MDOT
(Michigan Department of Transportation) wouldn't be here putting their money into
the airport. Mr. Lounsbury urged the Board of Commissioners to accept the grant
being offered by MDOT later in the meeting.

PHYSICAL RESOURCES AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

 

It was moved by Commissioner Gilmore, seconded by Commissioner Fancon to defund
the Clare County Airport for the upcoming year. Commissioner Ambrozaitis called for
a point of order citing the motion was not on the agenda. Chairman Haskell called for
discussion. Commissioner Gilmore gave his opinion that the Airport is a huge drain
on the county. Commissioner Ambrozaitis confirmed that since 1980 there have only
been 2 appropriations, totaling $30,000.00. Commissioners Ambrozaitis and Hoefling
reminded the Board of Commissioners that there are many signed hanger leases, an
airport restaurant lease and 14 airport restaurant employees to consider.
Commissioner Gilmore indicated the county attorney could handle breaking the
leases. Commissioner Fancon offered his opinion that the airport only benefits 1-3
townships in the county and questioned what value it has brought to Clare County.

PHYSICAL RESOURCES AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT - continued

 

Commissioner Ambrozaitis confirmed that the Clare County Airpert has been in
existence for 88 years and the current Airport Committee has set forth a plan, for the
first time ever, to improve the airport with donated items from other airports and grant
money from the State of Michigan. Commissioner Ambrozaitis pointed out that at this
point, when improvements are being made and a plan is in place, Commissioners
Gilmore and Fancon presented a motion to defund and airport with plans of closing it.
There have been communication with investors to build new hangers in an effort to
start businesses in Clare County, but investors don’t want to build if there are threats
of breaking leases and closing the airport. Commissioner Ambrozaitis confirmed one
hanger was just purchased with the intent of opening an aviation related business.

Chairman Haskell pointed out that the 2025 budget has not been presented or
approved and believes that a motion to defiund something that has not been funded is
premature and asked Commissioner Gilmore to rescind his motion. Commissioner
Gilmore and Commissioner Fancon agreed to withdraw their motion and have this
issue put on the agenda for the Committee of the Whole.
