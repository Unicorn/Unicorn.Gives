FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

1. Bond Discussion
Road Commission, Members of the Public and Bond Counsel
There was a question regarding what was approved at the last meeting. Eric
McGlothlin of Dickenson Wright Bond Counsel explained to the Board of
Commissioners that what they approved at the last meeting was simply a
Resolution for Notice (publication} of Intent to Issue Bonds.

Al Demarest, resident of Clare for 78 years a concerned taxpayer explained
he was not criticizing the way the Commissioners Manage the funds of Clare
County from their chairs, he only wanted to express his opinion. Mr.
Demarest believes that the Commissioners passed resolutions and
conceptually approved road commission project. Mr. Demarest feels that
this issue needs to be taken to the people in the form ofa referendum. He
explained that a $12 million loan at 4.36% (30 year amortization) would end
with payment of in excess of $10 million in interest, $730,000.00 each year
in payments and will be the #1 item budgeted for each year.

Dewayne Rogers, Road Commission Manager indicated that he has been
allocating over a million dollars a year for maintenance on the failing
building and will be able to sustain the payment required for this bond.
They have been replacing equipment and have a rotation plan now and will
not be required to replace 6 pieces of equipment at a time as has been
needed in the recent past. There are currently 18 trucks and the plan will
replace 1 truck a year with the new rotation plan.

FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION - continued
1. Bond Discussion — continued

Having a new facility and being able to store, wash and maintain the
equipment properly will make the equipment last longer.

Mr. McGlothlin explained risk and liability for Clare County - The County
makes a legal pledged security to bond holders and the bonds would be paid
out of the MTF revenues (MTF is the gas tax, license and registration fees
paid to Road Commissions) — This form of budgeting debt services out of
monies received has been broadly used throughout municipalities for many
years.

Manager Rogers answered the question “Could the Road Commission wait 5-
years and save money toward the building” - No, repair costs for the failing
building will increase as the years pass along with the increase in the cost of
building.

It was explained that the current facility is not ADA compliant, the women
have to use the men’s bathroom with the men. The new building will resolve
these issues and provide front office, a meeting room, new shop facility for
mechanics which includes a wash bay that they don’t have now, a larger
mechanic bay for larger trucks, a parking garage that will accommodate all
vehicles. It will not include new office furniture, they will continue to use
the same desks, chairs and filing cabinets they currently use.

Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger stated that she has managed the road
commission funds for the past 16 years and confirmed that they stay within
their budget and have a great healthy fund balance. Treasurer Beemer-
Fritzinger feels comfortable the Road Commission will be able to make the
required payments on this proposed bond.

Manager Rogers confirmed that every time each of us fill up gas tanks or
register our car, we are paying on a bond for Isabella County Road
Commission and a $65 million bond for Kalamazoo County. The money
each of us spends is not specific to Clare County just because we spend it in
Clare County.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Dan Dysinger, Grant Township Supervisor is in favor of a millage to the taxpayers. He
believes the Road Commission will just need to educate the public and believes that
this long term indebtedness is disastrous.

Pat Adams confirmed he has been to many Clare County Road Commission meetings
to hear the discussion and no one likes to pay interest, but there are safety concerns
and non-ADA compliant issues, this is not 100% a money issue. A new building is a
necessity and should not be put off for extended time.
