FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

1. Clerk-Election Reporting Discussion
Clerk Lori Mott explained to the Board of Commissioners that she ordered
an election night reporting software in January and the vendor promptly
went to work to ensure the software would be ready for the February
Election. In late February the vendor sent a contract to be signed. Clerk
Mott did not use the software in February because the contract needed to be
approved by the Board of Commissioners. When presented to the Board of
Commissioners in March of 2024 there was a tie vote, which results in a
failed motion.

PUBLIC HEARINGS /SPECIAL PRESENTATIONS

1. Patrick Tedham, Advantage Benefits Group
Mr. Tedham was not present, Mr. Mike Cutlip from Advantage Benefits
Group gave a presentation on the Advantage Benefits Group and the
benefits and cost savings with the different plans being offered.

2. Commissioner Ambrozaitis - Airport Presentation
Commissioner Ambrozaitis gave a presentation of the Clare County Airport
and discussed some of the items the Airport Committee has been able to
accomplish in 2024, which inchided new updated leases for the restaurant
and hangers. Commissioner Ambrozaitis outlined the short range plan —
fixing cracked runways, removing gravel from runway C and finish existing
airport terminal. The Airport Committee and pilots are trying to put together
events and fundraisers to raise money and bring in more airport traffic.
Commissioner Ambrozaitis invited everyone to visit the new website for the
airport - www.harrisonapa.org

ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps confirmed she is working on contract negotiations with 6 of
the 9 union units and the 2025 budget. Administrator Phelps gave an update
regarding funding for counties included in the Governor’s Budget. She then reported
that out of 14 counties, 7 SPARKS Grants were awarded, 3 of those awards being to
Clare County with Hayes Township receiving $722,500.00, Lincoln Township received
$772,700.00 and the City of Harrison for $757,700.00. Administrator Phelps
applauded Hayes Township, Lincoln Township and the City of Harrison on their hard
work in obtaining these competitive grants.

TREASURER’S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners and reported that the first 2024 land sale will be on August 1, 2024
and will include 28 properties. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger reported that the 2024
property tax bills have been mailed out along with the delinquent tax notices and
confirmed that Board of Review has been completed.
Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger confirmed that she worked into the evening hours the
night before with the Clerk performing the 6-hour long Public Accuracy Test on the
election equipment, program cards and ballots that will be used in the Clare County
Early Voting in August.

CHAIRMAN’'S REPORT

Chairman Jeff Haskell reported that the Clare County unemployment rate is 5.7% and
reminded everyone that the Harrison Street Fair is in August.

VICE CHAIRMAN’S REPORT
Vice Chairman Dale Majewski has been attending his meetings and spoke about the
Sparks Grant awarded to Lincoln Township in the amount of $772,700.00.
Commissioner Majewski also confirmed that the tornado that touched down over the
July 4th weekend was rated EF1 and most of the damage was east of Old State Road.
