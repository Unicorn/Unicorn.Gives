PHYSICAL RESOURCES

1. EV Charging Station Discussion and Contract Review
There would be 2 spaces with 2 parking spots each at no cost to Clare
County and would receive $0.03 per kilowatt. The Company will maintain
and service chargers. Commissioners questioned the following sections of
the contract:

What is the authority of the County Commissioners to stop non-electric cars
from parking in the electric vehicle space as noted in the contract? It was
agreed that the County Security/Sheriff will enforce.

Clarification is needed on page 4 of paragraph 12, it should spell out that
maintenance of site equipment should be maintained by CEV (Charge EV,
LLC}.

If the Company goes bankrupt and closes it appears the liability of removing
the stations would be on the County.

Administrator Phelps will get clarification on these sections of the contract
and put this item on the agenda at the next meeting.

2. Hayes Township Blight Contract Discussion
Administrator Phelps noted that Commissioner Gilmore had concerns
regarding the contract and the attorney made requested changes regarding
liability of litigation. Commissioner Ambrozaitis requested to see the
changes; Administrator Phelps will get the original contract to the
Commissioners and outline the changes.

3. Weapons Policy
55th Circuit Court Judge Tara Hovey presented the Weapons Policy to the
Board of Commissioners. Judge Hovey noted that there is already a
Weapons Policy in place and the major change would be that employees and
attorneys would be required to enter through security instead of bypassing,
as is currently being done. The Board of Commissioners had ne questions.

4. Airport Crack/Seal and Paint Contract
Commissioner Ambrozaitis presented the crack, seal and paint contract to
the Board of Commissioners. MDOT (Michigan Department of
Transportation) has excess recovery funds and has offered a no match grant
to Clare County. The contract allocates $19,952.60 for crack sealing and
$28,588.80 for paint marking of the Airport Runway, at no cost to Clare
County.

PHYSICAL RESOURCES - continued

4. Airport Crack/Seal and Paint Contract - continued
Commissioner Gilmore questioned if those funds would have to be paid back
if the Board of Commissioners closed the Airport. Commissioner
Ambrozaitis confirmed that the funds would not have to be paid back.
Commissioner Ambrozaitis went on to state that the County Airport is an
economic development engine for Clare County and Harrison to continue to
bring and attract business for Clare County and Hayes Township and
recommended this item be brought forward to the next meeting for vote.
The contract will be on the next meeting agenda.

FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION - continued

2. Redding Township Property Offer Discussion
Kari Garber from Harrison Realty presented the offer to purchase the
Redding Township property in the amount of $25,000.00. Ms. Garber
confirmed that the market value of the property would be $32,500 -
$34,500.

It was moved by Commissioner Ambrozaitis, seconded by Commissioner
Hoefling to make a counter-offer in the amount of $32,000.00, motion
carried.

3. Conservation District Property Purchase
Administrator Phelps confirmed that Clare County leases a 12-acre parcel
off from M-61 for $1.00 per year. The Conservation District has the
opportunity to receive a grant for the property, but cannot accept the grant
unless they own the property or have a 99-year lease agreement. It was
agreed that a 99-year lease agreement will be brought to the next board
meeting for $1.00 per year.

4. Courtroom Seating Discussion
Administrator Phelps brought to the Commissioners attention that she has
been locking for an alternative to the current benches in the courtrooms.
The current benches are in disrepair and need to be replaced.
Administrator Phelps found on Facebook Marketplace pews for sale that
would work well as replacements for the current benches. Administrator
Phelps would normally just make the purchase, but wanted to alert the
Board of Commissioners to the purchase because the pews were listed for
sale by the County Clerk/Register of Deeds and wanted to make sure the
purchase was made transparent. No one opposed the purchase of pews.

5. Airport Lease Increase Discussion
The current lease agreements were renewed in 2022 for a period of 7-years
and indicated no increase in the first and last year. The lease was increased
by 5% last year and Commissioner Ambrozaitis proposes another increase of
5% this year based on the Airport Committee approval. The 5% increase
this year would be $14.03 total new rate of $294.81 effective July 1, 2024.
This item will be moved to the agenda at the next meeting.

It was moved by Commissioner Haskell, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling to
authorize Ms. Garber to negotiate the highest offer she can get for the piece of property
in Redding Township, motion carried.

Meeting adjourned at 10:38 a.m.
