Meeting was called to order at 9:00 a.m. by Vice-Chairman Dale Majewski.

District 1 Marlene Housler, District 2 Dale Majewski, District 3 Samantha Pitchford,
District 5 David Hoefling, District 6 Jack Kleinhardt, District 7 Gabe Ambrozaitis and

It was moved by Commissioner Housler, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling to
approve the agenda as presented, motion carried.

JUSTICE

1. Closed Session - Labor Relations
It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner
Hoefling to go into closed session under section 8(e) to discuss litigation and
or settlement strategy in International Union, United Automobile, Aerospace
and Agricultural Implement Workers of America UAW vs. Clare County
Courts, roll call vote revealed:

Yeas: (7) being Commissioners Majewski, Hoefling, Housler, Pitchford,
Kleinhardt. Ambrozaitis and Gilmore

Nays: {0)

Absent: (2) being Commissioners Haskell and Fancon

Motion carried

Entered into closed session at 9:03 a.m.
Back in open session at 9:53 a.m.

It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner
Hoefling to approve the settlement agreement between the International
Union, United Automobile, Aerospace and Agricultural Implement Workers
of America UAW vs. Clare County Courts, roll call vote revealed:

Yeas: (4) being Commissioners Majewski, Hoefling, Housler, and Ambrozaitis
Nays: (3) being Commissioners Gilmore, Kleinhardt and Pitchford

Absent: (2) being Commissioners Haskell and Fancon

Motion carried

2. Honorable Chief Judge Farrell - Security
Judge Farrell commended the Board of Commissioners for the consistent
security progress Clare County has made in securing the Clare County
Building for the community and employees.

Judge Farrell then presented a new policy complied from several different
counties and asked the Board of Commissioners to adopt this new policy, as
Gladwin County already has adopted.

JUSTICE - continued

2. Honorable Chief Judge Farrell - Security - continued
In the new policy, Judge Farrell recommended Clare County follow the
Supreme Courts recommendations to scan all employees and delivery
persons entering the building, and went on to site that in the last 30 days
Gladwin County discovered 2-knives and 1 firearm using the new scanner
system and Clare County detected 1-knife last week using their new
scanning system. Judge Farrell then confirmed that the Board of
Commissioners could exempt anyone from the scanning procedures of their
choosing. This itern will be placed on a future Board of Commissioner
Agenda.

3. Jury Board Discussion
County Clerk Lori Mott informed the Board of Commissioner that at the next
Board of Commissioner's meeting she will be asking them to appoint
Therese Haley to the Jury Board and confirmed that Ms. Haley has
represented the Democrat Party as a Jury Board Member since 2016 and
her term has expired, raising the need for re-appointment.

FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

1. Redding Township Property sale discussion
Administrator Phelps asked if the Board of Commissioners would like her to
place this County owned Redding Township property on the market for sale.
Commissioner Ambrozaitis asked Administrator Phelps to get an appraisal of
the property as part of the process. Administrator Phelps also confirmed
she will ask 3 different real estate agents to submit their lowest commission
bid for selling the property. This item will be placed on the next Agenda.

2. Millage Renewals
Administrator Phelps confirmed there are 4 millage renewals that will need
to be voted on for renewal if the Commissioner’s want these millages
renewed. The only difference is that the renewals will be for 6-years because
of the way elections are scheduled. The 4 millage renewals are spongy
moth, animal control, senior citizens services and 911/Central Dispatch
Services. All 4 millage renewals will be placed on the agenda at the next
Board of Commissioner's Meeting.

Meeting adjourned at 10:34 a.m.
