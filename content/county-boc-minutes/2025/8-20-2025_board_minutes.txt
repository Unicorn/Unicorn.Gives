TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the updated statutory reports to the
Commissioners. She began her report by providing clarification in regards to a letter to
the editor that caused some confusion regarding the County foreclosing on families.
Some are thinking that the County foreclosed on 50 families and that’s not the

case. The Treasurer’s office works really hard with people to not have to foreclose
especially those with families. 50 parcels were foreclosed out of approximately 37,000
- that is 0.137%. That means that 99.863% of the people with properties in Clare
County are able to pay their taxes. She reminded everyone that the County’s first
public land sale will take place online this Friday, August 22, 2025 at tax-sale.info.

PUBLIC COMMENT

A Clare County resident expressed concern that she read an article indicating that
some Commissioners had requested additional time to review the budget prior to its
approval. Despite those requests, the budget was still passed ahead of schedule.
Chairman Gilmore responded that all Commissioners had been given a month to
prepare and emphasized that the approval process was not rushed.
