GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

A citizen expressed concern regarding the optics of increasing the Prosecuting
Attorney’s wages after he expressed satisfaction with his current wage. Chairman
Gilmore explained that the Wage and Personnel Committee recently conducted a wage
study and it was determined that the wage was slightly under average in comparison
to surrounding counties. Therefore, the committee decided to take him up to average
over a three year period. Additionally, the citizen expressed concern regarding the
former prosecuting attorney’s lawsuit against Clare County. Chairman Gilmore replied
that the Board of Commissioners is meeting with the attorney today in a closed
session.

ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps provided the Commissioners with an update regarding the
Cyber Security Audit that has been ongoing for some time, it will be complete within
the next week. She has been working with Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger and the Bond
counsel to finalize information for the Road Commission. She also mentioned that
Charter/Spectrum is expanding their fiber print in the county. The plan set last year
to update all Windows systems wili be complete by October 14, 2025. An update from
the Governor’s office came through stating that counties will receive a proposed 4%
increase in revenue sharing, however it does not apply to the entire revenue sharing
base from 2025.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Cierk & Register of Deeds Lori Mott announced that Clare-Gladwin RESD, Osceola
County ISD and Hayes Township all filed renewal millages for the May 6, 2025
Election. This will be countywide, except for Redding Township. Redding Township
does not have any of these items in their district.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the updated statutory reports to the
Commissioners. The Treasurer’s office is busy with all three current tax deadlines and
dog license renewais. Notices were recently sent out to potential claimants whose
property or interest was foreclosed and sold in an auction from 2013-2020. This keeps
in line with the new Supreme Court ruling in Schafer v Kent County. The deadline to
file a notice of intent to claim the possible proceeds is March 31, 2025.

Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger announced another one-stop shop rabies vaccination and
dog license clinic will take place February 22, 2025 at the animal shelter for Clare
County residents.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Chairman George Gilmore had nothing to report, but clarified with Attorney Bonny
Toskey regarding the county policy on contacting her office. The policy reads that
board members may contact her office regarding board business and requests from
department heads and elected officials should contact the Administrator first.
Attorney Toskey confirmed that would be correct if that is what the county policy
states.

STATE OF THE COUNTY BUDGET

Administrator Phelps reported that the budget should be 41.67% expensed. It is
currently 31.4% expensed. Last year at this time it was 38.57% expensed. Clare
County has received 14% of all revenue but that is not unusual this time of year.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Lisa Ashley, the Recovery Court Director, gave special recognition to Treasurer Jenny
Beemer-Fritzinger for her help with the program fees she collects, to Clerk & Register
of Deeds Lori Mott for her Chief Deputy Clerk Stacy Pechacek’s invaluable help as a
member of the Clare County Recovery Court team and also to Administrator Lori
Phelps for keeping up with the recovery court budget and helping when needed. Ms.
Ashley reiterated to the Board of Commissioners that they have a really great staff and
elected officials working for Clare County.

NEW BUSINESS

Zoom Discussion

The Commissioners had a discussion on whether they want to continue to zoom the
Board of Commissioners’ meetings. A couple commissioners mentioned that due to
distance and time of meetings that some members of the public are not able to attend
the meetings in person so the Zoom recordings are necessary. It was also brought up
that posting the meetings on the website again would be a benefit to the community
that cannot take time during the middle of the day to watch and could watch at their
convenience. The Commissioners will continue this discussion at the March 5, 2025
Committee of the Whole meeting.

It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner Housler to
adjourn the meeting at 11:15 a.m., motion carried.
