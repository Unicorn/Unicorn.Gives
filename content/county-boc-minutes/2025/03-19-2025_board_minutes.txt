GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

A member of the public made a comment regarding several out of budget items that
have historically been purchased for the county. He asked if the Board would consider
a budget adjustment to pay the outstanding 2023 Airport crack and seal invoice so the
County Airport does not lose an awarded grant because of the unpaid invoice.
Chairman Gilmore replied that the County Administrator is currently handling all the
Airport Manager duties so there is no threat on the airport closing. They are waiting
for an appointment with the Aeronautics Association to discuss their options. The
member of the public also asked if a decision was made to pursue or not pursue legal
action against the previous prosecutor to pay the legal fees for the lawsuit that she
brought against the County and Chairman Gilmore responded that the Board of
Commissioners decided not to proceed.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk & Register of Deeds Lori Mott gave an update regarding the upcoming May 6,
2025 Special Election. The election ballots have been printed and announced that she
was proud that Clare County was third in the state to complete ballot programming
and place their ballot order. Clerk & Register of Deeds Mott also confirmed the local
clerks will have their ballots within a week. She also shared that she has reached out
to schedule some dedicated time with Clare County Senior Services and Clare County
Veterans Affairs in an effort to answer questions for their respective clients regarding
the services the Clerk’s office offers.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners including the current cash on hand, investments, and fund balances
as of March 18, 2025 and the cash summary by funds for the month of February. She
reported that her office is working on reconciling $57 million in 2024 property taxes
and special assessments with the townships, cities, and village. They are 90%
collected, which means only a 10% delinquency rate which is the status quo. The
dollar amount delinquent that they will be purchasing to make all the taxing
authorities whole is approximately $5.5 million. The county millage specifically levied
with all tax adjustments accounted for is $6.8 million.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Chairman George Gilmore reported that Sheridan Township residents are being
recruited for solar production.

NEW BUSINESS

1. Drain Board Internal Loan
Treasurer Jenny gave an overview on the Clare County drain work that is set
to be completed countywide. She reminded the Commissioners that the
Drain Board is its own entity and they can take out their own bonding and
financing however, it does have to go through the Clare County Board of
Commissioners for final approval to sign the contracts. The Drain Board
