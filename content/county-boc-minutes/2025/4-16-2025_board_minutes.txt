GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

A member of the public expressed his gratitude for the new speaker and microphone
system. He expressed his concern regarding the lack of attendance for one of the
Commissioners that sits on the Airport Committee. Also, he stated that in the previous
meeting he requested a press release in regard to the total costs of all Clare County
lawsuits since the information was being released regarding the former prosecutor’s
lawsuit. Chairman Gilmore responded that the prosecutor lawsuit was the first lawsuit
that was taken out of the General fund, the other lawsuits were paid for by insurance
money. Additionally, the same member of the public mentioned that he spoke to
Prosecuting Attorney Andrew Santini in regard to his staffing and Prosecuting
Attorney Santini clarified that he has three attorneys working in the office and the
previous Prosecuting Attorney had a total of four.

The previous Airport Manager asked the Board of Commissioners to please support
the Airport. He pleaded with the Commissioners to set up joint funding with the City
of Harrison and Hayes Township so the Airport may have a stable source of income.

A member of the public questioned what the public benefit is for having an airport is
for the community.

A citizen concerned about the airport asked the Board of Commissioners to consider
accepting the Airport Grant of $254,000.00. He mentioned that the Federal Aviation
Administration has already designated the funding for airports so if the
Commissioners do not accept the grant it will just go to another airport and
community.

ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps reported that there are no legislative updates and a few
federally funded programs are on hold. The fiscal year 2026 budget template has been
released to all the department heads for completion. The final audit was received on
March 31, 2025 and once all the audit reports are completed, she will post them on
the transparency page on the county website. She is working on streamlining the
process regarding inmate health insurance with Blue Cross Blue Shield and confirmed
that County Building emergency system tests (including tornado and fire drills) were
recently conducted with successful results. Administrator Phelps reported that she is
reviewing and updating several outdated policies, and she sent out several letters of
support for organizations applying for grants over the past month. She closed by
confirming that the County is a little under budget but expects some large invoices to
be submitted in the coming weeks.

PUBLIC HEARINGS/SPECIAL PRESENTATIONS

1. Region VII Area Agency on Aging
Chief Operating Officer and interim Executive Director Monica Freier gave a
presentation on the various services and programs Region VII Area Agency
offers the senior citizens of Clare County. She explained that Region VII Area
Agency serves 10 counties in the state under the Older Americans Act. The
agency distributes funds that come in through federal and state grants.
Monica Freier pointed out that in 2024 Clare County paid $2,474 in dues
yet received $418,000 in return. The MDHHS and ACLS Administration for
Community Living Support are working with all the Region Area Agencies to
make sure all available funds are being distributed in a timely manner.

Monica Freier also explained that they are currently lobbying for the MI
Choice Waver Program. The program provides in-home services for nursing
facility level of care citizens keeping them where they want to be while still]
receiving the services needed. Last year 161 residents of Clare County
benefitted from the MI Choice Waver Program. She encouraged the
Commissioners to send senior citizens to Region VII Area Agency. Finally,
she emphasized that they have a strong contingency plan in the rare
occasion that funding ceased.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott gave an update regarding the upcoming May
election and confirmed the filing deadline for the August election is May 13, 2025. She
also reported that the Veterans Coffee hour was a success and there were several
veterans that signed up for the Property Fraud Alert.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. She also included the final numbers for Fiscal Year 2024 because the
audit is now complete, which showed Clare County ended with an addition of
$862,791.00 to the Fund balance. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger noted that 2024
included $800,000 of ARPA funds. Settlement checks will be going out next week to
the local townships and cities and her staff worked hard through the third tax
deadline, which inchuded 1,024 certificates of forfeiture for the 2023 tax year that were
sent over to the Register of Deeds office for recording.
