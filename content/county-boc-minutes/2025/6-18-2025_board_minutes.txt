COMMISSIONER’S REPORT

Commissioner Hoefling has attended his meetings and gave a report on each of the
townships and committees he represents.

Commissioner Gross reported on the Farwell Lumberjack Festival scheduled for July
and has had a lot of questions from constituents regarding the 911 rescue response
problem in Clare County and finally reported that he will be attending his Substance
Abuse Disorder Policy Board Meeting that evening with MidState in Lansing.

Commissioner Ambrozaitis reported that 5 members of the Michigan Army National
Guard spent some time at the County Airport to perform a survey and design of the
County Airport. There is a federal program called Innovative Readiness Training. The
military needs to do training and they need new and unique innovative ways to do that
training. This unit came from Ishpaming in the Upper Peninsula and provided this
survey and design free of charge for the training experience. Commissioner
Ambrozaitis tried to get local contractors to bid finishing the County Airport Terminal
without success so he asked this unit to provide the design and build packet. They
wili also look at the drainage on the Airport, the South entrance, the West entrance,
the West perimeter road, the taxiways, the turf runway and design a new runway.
Those survey and design build packets are then provided to units that will come and
build the projects free of labor charges. The only charge would be for materials and
fuel used onsite. Approval could be in the next 4-5 months from the military. This
airport project provides needed training for these military units. One of the units is
tasked with building and maintaining war time airports and this is a great training
opportunity for them.

Commissioner Haskell reported the unemployment rate is 8.4% for Clare County.

Commissioner Kleinhardt reported that it looks like the revenue sharing is on track to
stay the same, but Medicaid cuts may be coming.

Commissioner Majewski had no report
