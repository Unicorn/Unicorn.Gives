JUSTICE

1. Life Saving Award Presentation
Undersheriff Ed Williams presented Sergeant James Dawson with the Clare
County Sheriffs Department Bravery Award for his unselfish bravery
demonstrated when rescuing a 12 year old girl during a standoff in Coleman
on February 18, 2025.

2. Honorable Judge Josh Farrell and Honorable Judge Tara Hovey
Judge Farrell explained to the Board of Commissioners that a task from the
Michigan Supreme Court to the Clare County Courts to modify the entire
structure of the court system will impact staffing significantly. The shift will
increase the Circuit Court Judges caseloads which will affect the labor load
of the Circuit Court staff. Judge Farrell proposes that the Commissioners
consider bringing everything proportionate to what the caseload suggests.
Judge Hovey added that essentially they are trying to fund a Circuit Court
clerk position to handle the load of cases. Simply moving a clerk from
Probate Court to Circuit Court is complicated due to union, audit and
additional issues.

3. Off Road Vehicle (ORV) Ordinance
Administrator Lori Phelps presented the updated ORV Ordinance for the
Commissioners to review and approve at the next regular meeting. Dewayne
Rogers, Clare County Road Commission Manager, asked if an amendment
would be possible to allow ORV operators to use the entire road versus
operating the ORV with one tire on the road and one tire on the
gravel/shoulder area. This would help alleviate the current erosion issues
caused by ORV operators driving partly on the gravel/shoulder area of the
road.

HUMAN AND SOCIAL SERVICE

. Senior Services Loan Payoff Request

Administrator Phelps asked for the Board of Commissioners to consider a
low interest internal loan through the Delinquent Tax Fund and the General
Fund to be paid off in five years. Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger added
that the Harrison building loan is payable through 2033 and this internal
loan would save years of interest and would be more beneficial for seniors in
the long run.

. Veterans Annual Update

Karl Hauser, Director of Clare County Veterans Services, provided an annual
update of the programs and services offered to Clare County veterans. He
highlighted that his office is not a part of the Department of Veterans
Affairs, as this is often a matter of confusion.

FINANCE AND ADMINISTRATION

 

. Zoom Discussion

Chairman Gilmore opened the discussion on whether or not the use of Zoom
is still necessary. A point was made that Zoom provides an opportunity for
the members of the public who cannot attend to still be aware of what is
discussed at the Board of Commissioners meetings. A decision will be made
at the March 19, 2025 meeting to continue or discontinue Zoom.

. Night Meeting Discussion

The Commissioners went into discussion regarding the possibility of
conducting periodic night meetings. A couple Commissioners expressed
concern with night meetings due to their schedules already being filled up
with other meetings held in the evenings. Another possibility discussed is to
eliminate the Committee of the Whole meeting and extending the time length
of the regular meeting. The final decision will be voted on at the March 19,
2025 meeting.

. Soil Erosion and Spongy Moth Contracts

Administrator Lori Phelps explained that the Soil Erosion and Spongy Moth
Contracts with the Conservation District are up for renewal. The contracts
will be action items on the next Board of Commissioners meeting.

PUBLIC COMMENT

Bob Dodson, Director of the Clare County Animal Shelter, shared the 2024 yearly
report.

A member of the public from Hatton Township expressed his concern on the
transparency and accountability regarding the former prosecutor’s lawsuit. He asked
what the total cost of the lawsuit was and who will end up paying for it. The public
should be made aware of the full time and cost.

A question was asked regarding how the members of the public are made aware that
the meetings are available to attend via Zoom.

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger mentioned that potentially there will be another
loan payoff request in regard to the Drain Department. The Drain Board approved an
internal loan from the Little Tobacco Drain to upfront all the drain work.

A final question was asked regarding Cranberry Lake and whether or not a special
assessment would have to be made to pay back the drain work. Administrator Lori

Phelps replied that it has already been done.

It was moved by Commissioner Housler, seconded by Commissioner Fancon to
adjourn the meeting at 11:09 a.m., motion carried.
