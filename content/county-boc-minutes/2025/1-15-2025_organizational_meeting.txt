APPROVAL OF AGENDA

It was moved by Commissioner Ambrozaitis, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling to
approve the agenda as presented. Clerk Mott asked for discussion, hearing none,
motion carried.

ELECTION OF CHAIRMAN

Clerk Mott called for nominations for the election of the Board of Commissioners
Chairman three times. Commissioner Gilmore and Commissioner Haskell were
nominated. It was moved by Commissioner Ambrozaitis, seconded by Commissioner
Haskell to vote by secret ballot, motion carried. Commissioner Gilmore was elected
for the Board of Commissioners Chairman.

ELECTION OF VICE-CHAIRMAN

Chairman Gilmore called for nominations of the Board of Commissioners Vice-
Chairman three times. Commissioner Majewski nominated Commissioner Fancon,
hearing no other nominations Chairman Gilmore closed nominations, Commissioner
Fancon was unanimously elected as Vice-Chairman.

COMMITTEE APPOINTMENTS

Chairman Gilmore passed out committee appointments that he drafted in anticipation
of being elected Chairman that will replace the committee assignments made by
Commissioner Haskell and announced that the committee appointments will take
place at the next Committee of the Whole meeting.

BOARD RULES

It was moved by Commissioner Ambrozaitis, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling to
update Section 8.2 on page 20 to make it the next general election, motion carried.

It was moved by Commissioner Majewski, seconded by Commissioner Housler to adopt

the board rules with the changes recommended by Commissioner Ambrozaitis,
motion carried.

GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

A citizen expressed concern regarding leakage from garbage trucks, black substances
covering their roof, a GFL truck dropping garbage bags in the street, another truck
losing his cargo, apparent spills of black substance in the roadway in their
neighborhood and showed photographs of each. Chairman Gilmore replied that the
county or township does not have jurisdiction over the landfili and offered the
concerned citizen the phone number to the State Representative.

as his replacement after his retirement in February, Jeremy Switzer the new
Operations Manager and Rachell Ramirez the new Office Manager.

4. Bond Resolution
Discussion was initiated by Chairman Gilmore on whether the Board should
pass a motion to table and have a meeting at the Road Commission so all the
Commissioners can be more informed. It was determined that every
Commissioner has had the opportunity to visit the Road Commission and
waiting would hinder the timeline that Dewayne Rogers, Clare County Road
Commission Manager, has already set. The Road Commission Board is already
in full support.

It was moved by Commissioner Kleinhardt, seconded by Commissioner Hoefling
to adopt Resolution #25-05, authorizing issuance of the County’s Limited Tax
General Obligation Bonds in the maximum principal amount of $12,000,000.00
to finance Clare County Road Commission facilities improvements and pledging
the County’s limited tax full faith and credit to repayment of such bonds, roll
cali revealed:

Yeas: (8) being Commissioners Kleinhardt, Hoefling, Housler, Ambrozaitis, Gross,
Fancon, Haskell and Majewski

Nays: (1) being Chairman Gilmore

Absent: (0)

Motion carried

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk & Register of Deeds Lori Mott gave an update regarding the reallocation of
Circuit Court Family Division cases being moved from Judge Klaus’ docket to Judge
Hovey and Judge Ambrozaitis’ docket, which are handled by the County Clerk’s office.

Clerk & Register of Deeds Mott announced that she expects a county wide election in
May and noted that the filing deadline is February 11, 2025.

TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger reported that the 2024 property taxes
are being collected at the townships, 2023 property taxes are in forfeiture and the
2022 property taxes will be foreclosed if not paid by March 1, 2025. Her office is
working hard to find and call property owners in an effort to help them avoid
foreclosure. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger reminded everyone that the dog license
deadline is February 28, 2025.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT
Chairman Gilmore explained to Dewayne Rogers, Clare County Road Commission
Manager, that his vote against adopting Resolution #25-05 is not personal, he voted

how his townships wanted him too and expressed his problems with the thirty-year
interest rate.
