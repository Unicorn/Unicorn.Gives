CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott informed the Board of Commissioners that the
TextMyGov program that is in use in her office is quite effective. The public can ask
questions via a text chat and Clerk/Register Mott can also push out notifications for
elections, election inspector training, etc. Information is available on the Clerk’s office
website. The 55th Circuit Court clerks have noticed an increase in Personal
Protections Orders (PPOs) along with an increase in the Family Court files. However,
there has been a decrease in jury trials, there has only been one jury trial so far this
year. The upcoming deadline for any ballot proposals for the November 2025 election
is August 12, 2025 at 4:00 p.m.

TREASURER’S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. She explained that one of the reports is the penal fine monies paid
out to libraries this time every year. The county’s first public land sale auction this
year will be online only on August 22, 2025.

CHAIRMAN’S REPORT

Chairman George Gilmore has been attending his meetings and there are no serious
issues.

VICE CHAIRMAN’S REPORT
Vice Chairman Rick Fancon gave a thanks to the Road Commission for their
completed project on Colonville Road.

COMMISSIONER’S REPORT
Commissioner Hoefling has attended his meetings and gave a report on each of the
townships and committees he represents. Frost Township expressed concerns about
the lack of timeliness of the ambulance service.
Commissioner Ambrozaitis had nothing to report.

Commissioner Housler had nothing to report.

Commissioner Kleinhardt expressed concern over the Axe the Tax movement. If the

Commissioner Haskell had nothing to report.

2025.

Commissioner Majewski had nothing to report.
