GENERAL PUBLIC COMMENT

Linda Barkel introduced herself as one of the candidates for the Republican Board of
Canvasser, which the Board of Commissioners will be voting on. She informed the
Commissioners that she is available to answer any questions.

A member of the public asked the Board of Commissioners what the difference in price
is between the estimate and the contract for the Airport Lighting Project.
Commissioner Ambrozaitis responded that the original was an estimate for the grant
approval and the subsequent number was the actual bid for the grant award.

COMMUNICATIONS LIST
None
ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps informed the Commissioners that the State budget is still
not approved. She and Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger have reviewed all State
funded allocations and have notified department heads of potential impacts should the
budget remain unsigned and a government shutdown occurs.

Additionally, Administrator Phelps announced that the County was awarded a
Materials Management Grant from Michigan’s Department of Environment, Great
Lakes and Energy (EGLE) in the amount of $80,000.00 for Fiscal Year 2026. The
$80,000 a year funding is expected for the next three years. Half of the Airport Grant
funding has been received totaling $113,000 and the crack seal project has been
completed.

Board of Canvassers

Deputy Clerk/Register of Deeds Walter read a statement from County
Clerk/Register of Deeds Lori Mott highlighting the questions asked of all
nominees and their answers. Clerk/Register Mott recommended Tara
Emmendorfer as the Democrat Canvasser because she was the only of the
three nominees that could commit to the time needed to be a Canvasser.
Clerk/Register Mott recommended Linda Barkell as the Republican
Canvasser because of her extensive knowledge and experience with
elections.

a. It was moved by Commissioner Ambrozaitis, seconded by

Democrat Board of Canvasser for the term of November 1, 2025
through October 31, 2029, Motion carried.

b. It was moved by Commissioner Housler, seconded by Commissioner
Fancon to appoint Dina Geer as the Republican Board of Canvasser
for the term of November 1, 2025 through October 31, 2029, Motion

TREASURER’S REPORT

Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the current statutory reports to the
Commissioners. She is working on fiscal year end with Administrator Phelps. She
reminded the Commissioners that she needs copies of all grants, contracts, and leases
that were signed in FY 2025 for accounting purposes. Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger
mentioned that they had their land sale on August 22, 2025 and the re-offer sale will
be online only on Friday, September 26, 2025 from 10 a.m. until 7 p.m. Finally, she
encouraged the Commissioners to reach out to their representatives regarding house
bill concerning effective postmark dates for property taxes.
