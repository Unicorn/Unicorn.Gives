CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Deputy Clerk Isha Walter read an update on behalf of Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori
Mott regarding the November 4, 2025 Special Election. The Harrison Community
Schools Bond Proposal was defeated. She informed the Board of Commissioners that
the Board of Canvassers are in the process of certifying the election, however, since
the passing of the Constitutional Amendment in 2022 all elections now take a week or
longer to be certified. She plans to bill the schools for the election later this week.
