ADMINISTRATOR’S REPORT

Administrator Lori Phelps reported that the annual audit is currently underway. The
State Treasury has taken an alternative approach with an expanded review. She also
informed the Board that 94 computers were replaced during the recent computer
replacement project, all computers are now up to date with the required programs.
She recognized Josh Chapman, Information Technology (IT) Director for his
outstanding work during the project.

Administrator Phelps will be opening the Fiscal Year 2027 budget at the end of
December and she has begun the bidding process for health insurance. A new Title 4D
funding policy requires all employees to read and acknowledge the policies and
entered into their personnel files annually. At the State level, the House approved work
projects cuts totaling $645 million in Fiscal Year 2025. Additionally, Administrator
Phelps informed the Commissioners that she prepared Lake Level books for each of
them and she will keep them updated.

CLERK/REGISTER OF DEEDS REPORT

Clerk and Register of Deeds Lori Mott reminded the Board of Commissioners of the
property fraud alert service offered to all Clare County residents. There is a current
ongoing investigation of property fraud in our county confirming that making Clare
County residents aware of the free service is beneficial. Clerk and Register of Deeds
Mott confirmed that work is already underway for 2026 elections and confirmed the
filing deadline for proposals for the August 2026 ballot. Clerk and Register of Deeds
Mott also informed the Board that the changes made by Judge Ambrozaitis and Judge
Hovey will result in her office staff calling 2,000 less jurors in 2026. She shared that at
her last conference recently a lobbyist spoke about the Ax MI Tax initiative and they
are confident it will not pass. Lastly, she reported on cost saving measures taken by
her office.

TREASURER’S REPORT
Treasurer Jenny Beemer-Fritzinger provided all the statutory reports to the
Commissioners. She reminded the Commissioners that because she was absent from
the November meeting she emailed them the November statutory reports. Winter tax
collection along with disbursements will be happening soon. The fifty drains and six
lake levels on the winter tax rolls will be challenging, the dog license deadline is
February 28, 2026, and Treasurer Beemer-Fritzinger mentioned that there will be a
one stop shop rabies clinic at the Animal shelter in February 2026. Lastly, she stated
that, as Clerk Mott mentioned, her office also takes cost saving measures.
CHAIRMAN’S REPORT
Chairman George Gilmore expressed his surprise regarding Grant Township’s recent
significant increase for liability insurance. He suggested other townships get new
estimates.
