\## Important framing note (spike finding)



The roadmap's "invoke the CA data-ownership right" needs a correction: there is no general

California statute granting a \*business\* the right to demand its data back from a SaaS

vendor (CCPA rights belong to individual consumers, not organizations). The real levers are:



1\. \*\*The Antaris contract's own data-ownership / export / termination clauses\*\* (strongest —

&#x20;  we need CYC's copy of the agreement to cite the exact section);

2\. \*\*The records are CYC's business data\*\* about its own members;

3\. \*\*Privacy-law shadow:\*\* the data includes personal information of California residents,

&#x20;  including minors, for which CYC bears ongoing responsibility.



The letter below leans on all three. Recommend legal review before sending if Antaris resists.
